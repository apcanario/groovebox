## Findings

### Errors

None.

### Should-fix

**S1. Four launch vibes' own chords are not in the library, so their Simple Chords shows no card Playing and Simple cannot get back to the vibe's chords.**
`src/styles/drumAndBass.ts`, `src/styles/loFi.ts`, `src/styles/melodicTechno.ts`: none of their section progressions is in the library; `src/styles/ambient.ts` (2 of 4), `src/styles/deepHouse.ts` (1 of 4) and `src/styles/twoStep.ts` (1 of 4) miss some. `findProgression` (`src/music/progressions.ts`) then finds nothing and one card tap replaces the vibe's chords for good in Simple.
Fix: add each missing vibe progression as a library file (data only), tagged with its vibe and a fitting feel, and add a test in `progressions.test.ts` that every section of every style in `STYLE_IDS` has its mood and chords in the library.

**S2. At 360 px the Chords title cuts off the page count.**
`src/ui/tabs/SimpleChords.tsx:115`: "B · Warm · 1 of…" ends in an ellipsis on `360x640-simple-chords.png`, so the page number (the only sign of how many More chords pages there are) is lost on the target phone width. The reference puts "DREAMY · 1 OF 2" on its own line above the cards.
Fix: move the feel and page line out of the title row onto its own line above the cards (as in groovebox-3), or drop the section letter from it so it fits at 360; keep it from being cut at 360 x 640.

**S3. Two assertions of the bar-line test pass when nothing plays.**
`src/engine/p4.test.ts:50` (`expect(bassNote(line) ?? first.bass).toBe(first.bass)`) and `:52` (`if (held) ...`) pass vacuously if the bar line carries no bass or synth event, so the test would not fail if the card's first chord were never played on the line.
Fix: make sure the section has bass and synth on, then assert unconditionally that the bar-line step has a bass event equal to `first.bass` and a synth event equal to `first.notes`.

**S4. No script checks that Simple Chords shows no scale or degree name.**
`tools/smoke.py:1635` (`walk_p4`): the first Done-when line is checked by eye only.
Fix: in `walk_p4`, read `inner_text` of the Chords tab in Simple and fail on any mood label (`MOOD_TABLE[*].label`), scale name, roman numeral (I to VII, either case, with or without °) or note name (C, C#, ..., B as a word). The section letter in the title (M3) must be handled, which is a reason to fix M3 too.

**S5. A card tapped while a vibe is loading shows "Next bar", then is silently dropped.**
`src/engine/sequencer.ts:468` (`setProgression`) queues while `nextSong` is set; at the bar line the loaded song clears the queue (`sequencer.ts:612`), so the card never plays though it showed as pending.
Fix: refuse `setProgression` (return false) while `songQueued`, and in `SimpleChords.tsx:93` disable the cards while `engine.pendingLoad()` is set; add a unit test.

### Minor

**M1. The library's in-scale assertion cannot fail.**
`src/music/progressions.test.ts:56`: `buildChord` builds every chord from the mood's scale, so `toContain(n % 12)` holds for any degree 0..6; the comment at `:50` says so. The same goes for the transposition check at `:63`. The real guard is `checkProgression` refusing degrees outside 0..6, which is tested.
Fix: keep it, but say in the test name that in-scale holds because only degrees are stored, and when L1 adds borrowed chords add a `borrowed` mark to `ProgressionDef` (`src/music/data.ts:60`) and the "or marked as borrowing" branch.

**M2. Vibe tags are six blanket groups, not per-vibe choices.**
`src/data/progressions/*.ts`: 123 of 126 progressions are tagged with 14 or 15 of the 24 vibes, mostly one of six identical lists (for example `alarm.ts` and `static.ts` both carry Drum and bass through Dub techno, 14 vibes). Every vibe meets the four-per-feel floor, but the cards hardly change between vibes, so borrowing another vibe's chords often shows the same cards.
Fix: when P8 writes the remaining vibes, narrow each progression's `vibes` to the ones it suits, keeping the four-per-feel test green.

**M3. The section letter beside the feel can read as a key.**
`src/ui/tabs/SimpleChords.tsx:115`: "B · Warm" on a chords screen looks like the key of B. 
Fix: drop the section letter from the Simple line (the strip already shows the playing section), or write it as "Section B".

**M4. A card sets the mood for the whole song.**
`src/engine/sequencer.ts:477` (`landChords`): the card's mood replaces the song's, so the other sections' chords now play in that mood (spec 3: one mood per song). This follows the spec, but it is a choice Pedro should see.
Fix: record it under Choices in the changelog line for P4.

**M5. At 360 px the layout differs from groovebox-3.**
`src/style.css:970` and `:991`: below 412 px the six feels sit in one row and the cards in a 2 x 2 grid; the reference (and the 412 px build) has two rows of three feels and full-width card rows. It fits and every control passes the 48 px check, but "Uplifting" is drawn small.
Fix: record it under Choices in the changelog, or use two rows of feels at 360 too if S2's line still fits.

**M6. The range test covers bass, synth and pad notes only.**
`src/music/chords.ts:17`: `CHORD_RANGE` is used only by the test; an arp at 2 or 3 octaves plays up to two octaves above it. This was already so before P4.
Fix: say in the comment at `chords.ts:14` that arp octaves are outside the range on purpose.

## Spec checks with nothing to report

Section 7: no UI import of Tone; the card lands through the sequencer's queue on the bar line, applied together with section switches and toggles (tested); the UI refresh at landing is `Tone.getDraw().schedule` at the audio time; no new randomness; the new pure logic (`progressions.ts`, queue additions) has unit tests. Stuck notes and clicks: a landing card sets `retrigger`, the same path as a chord start at a bar line; no new voices or parameter steps. Controls: smoke's `check_sizes` passes on every feel page and the borrow picker. Section 8.4 names: neutral words, no artist, song or label.

## Verdict

**fix**: 0 errors, 5 should-fix, 6 minor.
