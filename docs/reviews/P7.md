## Errors

**E1. A drone or swooping pad goes silent after two wave or patch changes, and the first change does not reach it.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 970 (`Poly.setWave`), 1133 (`glideTo`), with `VoiceSets.swap` at 463.
- `swap` keeps the previous set ringing, so held notes stay on the old wave. The second swap drops that set: it is ramped to 0 and disposed.
- `this.held` still points at the dropped set. `glideTo` then glides disposed voices, and no new notes start until a section change or Stop.
- Measured with layer 2 as a drone: -39.1 dB before, -38.0 dB after one wave change, -58.5 dB after two changes and two chord changes (`held` still reports 2).
- The same happens when a patch is picked with the new Sound picker (C8). `setPatch` goes through `setParams` and changes the wave.
- **Fix:** in `Poly.setWave`, when `held` has items, start the same notes on the new live set at the swap time, release the old ones with their release, and point `held` at the new set. Add a smoke check: drone on, two wave changes, a chord change, level still within 6 dB.

**E2. Dragging the Tail dial piles up reverbs and saturates the audio thread.** `src/engine/mods.ts` 289 (`setDecay`), 298 (`swap`).
- Each move more than 0.3 s after the last builds a new 3.2 s stereo convolver with its feedback loop.
- Each old one stays connected for `old.decay + 1` s, which is up to 21 s.
- Measured: 19 moves 0.35 s apart, from 20 s down to 4 s. Audio load went from 32.2% to 99.8% (worst callback 16.3 ms). That breaks spec 8.7's "within the phone CPU budget".
- **Fix:** keep at most two slots, the live one and one ringing. When a third is needed, fade the ringing one out over about 0.3 s and free it before building the new one. Add the drag to the cost scene in `tools/smoke.py`.

## Should-fix

**S1. Every vibe's reverb is now longer than before.** `src/engine/song.ts` 266 (`MIX_DEFAULTS.reverbDecay: 3.2`), `src/engine/audio.ts` 1239.
- G1's `Tone.Reverb({decay: 3.2})` shaped its impulse with `exponentialApproachValueAtTime`: a time constant of ln(4.2)/ln(200) = 0.27 s, so -60 dB at about 1.9 s.
- `reverbImpulse` now falls 60 dB over the full 3.2 s.
- Every song and vibe that does not set `reverbDecay` sounds about 1.7 times wetter in the tail.
- **Fix:** set the default to about 1.9 s in the new definition so nothing changes unasked, or record the change under Choices in the changelog for Pedro to veto.

**S2. The long-reverb feedback loop repeats a dip and the same noise every 3.2 s.** `src/music/sound.ts` 140 (the 10 ms fade-in), `src/engine/mods.ts` slot loop.
- Each pass through the loop replays the whole impulse, including its 10 ms fade-in. After a percussive hit, the tail has a near-gap of about 5 ms every 3.2 s. Gaps of 2 to 3 ms are audible in noise.
- The tail is also the same noise segment repeated, which can be heard as a cyclic texture.
- It is not a step click: the envelope is continuous apart from the dip.
- **Fix:**
  - shorten the fade-in to about 1 ms;
  - put a one-pole low-pass (about 5 to 6 kHz) inside the loop, so later passes darken the way a room does;
  - optionally, swing the loop delay by a fraction of a millisecond with a slow LFO to break the exact repeat.

**S3. Attack and release reach 20 s only on the Pad page.** `src/ui/logic.ts` 329-331 (Synth Sound dials: 2 s and 4 s), 572-575 (layer Sound dials: 2 s and 4 s).
- Spec 8.10 says "attack and release up to 20 seconds on pads and layers". Layers in Lead and Arp mode, and the Synth, still stop at 2 s and 4 s.
- Ambient sets the synth's attack to 3 s and release to 8 s (`src/styles/ambient.ts`), outside its dials. The dials show their maximum, and the first touch snaps the value down.
- **Fix:** give these dials the 20 s curve with seconds display (`secondsText`), like `PAD_KNOBS`. Or keep the vibe's values within the dials.

**S4. Smoke coverage misses what the Done-when lines need.** `tools/smoke.py` 2069 (`walk_p7`), 2121 (`switches`). Add:
- continuity checks for wave changes sawtooth -> fm -> noise -> sawtooth while a note holds;
- an LFO rate change while depth is above 0 (the fade-and-restart path);
- a drift rate change;
- drone -> arp -> drone while playing;
- Perc and Shaker level 0 reading silent, as the four G1 voices already do;
- the breath noise stopping once the wave leaves noise;
- E1's and E2's checks.

**S5. Disconnects are timed from now, not from `at`.** `src/engine/mods.ts` 31 (`afterFade`), used by `ModRoute.set`, `LoFi.set` (358) and `Air.update` (447); `afterRamp` in `Channel.setLfo` (236) and `setDrift` (283).
- When `at` is later than the context's lookahead, the node is cut before its fade starts, which clicks.
- It is safe today only because every `setMixAt` and `applyAt` time falls inside the lookahead.
- **Fix:** wait `max(at, now) - now` plus the fade.

## Minor

**M1. The cost measure is noisy.** `tools/cost.py`. Within three runs, load varies by ±5 points and the base's worst stall by 10 to 28 ms, so a median of 3 cannot separate a 6 ms stall difference. Run 7 or more, and report median, minimum and maximum for both builds. Building the base dist is a step the tool should document.

**M2. `ReverbBank.setDecay` ignores `at`.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 1278, `src/engine/mods.ts` 289. A vibe landing on the bar swaps its reverb about 0.3 s after the JS call, not on the audio clock. Pass `at` and start the crossfade there.

**M3. Drift moves pan, not width.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 211, 283. Spec 8.10 lists "width". Either swing the stereo spread (detune spread or a mid/side gain), or record "pan swing stands in for width" under Choices.

**M4. Values between 0 and 1 for `lofi` comb-filter.** `src/engine/mods.ts` 321/358. The wet path is delayed 8 ms and the dry path is not, so any value between 0 and 1 (Morph later, or the event log) gives comb filtering. Clamp `lofi` to 0 or 1 in `Graph.setMix`, or delay the dry path by the same 8 ms.

**M5. Off still costs a little.** `Lfo`'s `lift` ConstantSource (`src/engine/mods.ts` 71) never stops. The drift's pitch GainNode stays wired to every voice's detune after drift returns to 0, which keeps it pulled. Stop or disconnect both when depth is 0 (spec 8.8: off costs nothing).

**M6. The breath noise runs while stopped.** `src/engine/mods.ts` 166 (`Breath.setOn`). The noise source runs whenever the noise wave is selected, even with the transport stopped. Gate it on play, as `Air` and the lo-fi hiss do.

**M7. `Poly.unwire` does nothing.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 930. It is passed to `VoiceSets` as `onDrop`. Remove it, or make it do the unwiring.

**M8. The mode row differs from the look.** `src/style.css` 1512 (`.modes.withtog`), `LayerTab.tsx` `ModeRow`. With four modes plus On in one row at 360 px, "Drone" is drawn smaller than Lead, Arp and Pad. `docs/design/look/groovebox-v3-phone-3-advanced-layer.jpg` shows the four modes at full width with no switch in the row. Move On out of the row (to the head row, as in the look), or let the row wrap the switch.

**M9. Unrelated reformatting.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx` and `src/ui/tabs/LayerTab.tsx` reflow many lines the row does not change, which hides the real diff. Revert the formatting-only hunks.

## Notes (no change needed)

- C8 is done: a Sound picker per instrument in Advanced, and the Simple Synth's picker lists patches.
- The Ambient rework uses every spec 8.10 piece: 4- and 8-bar chords, a drone on Layer 2, Swoop, long pad times, drift, air and a 12 s reverb, at 64 bpm.
- Held notes let go correctly on Stop, on a song load, on an instrument toggled off, and on a mode or Swoop change.
- The 20 s reverb as a 3.2 s impulse plus feedback is stable (feedback = 10^(-3·3.2/d) < 1). The swap crossfades its input gates, so a single decay change makes no click.
