## Findings

### Errors

**E1. The ten other vibes' "length" is the gap to the next kick, not the kick's tail.** The test cannot fail on a changed tail. `tools/kickcheck.py:104`: `hi = ... int((nxt - t0) / span) + 1` takes in the block where the next kick starts. That onset is above the -30 dB floor, so every hit's "length" is the inter-onset gap plus one block:
- Tropical house: 0.5562 s against a gap of 0.5556 s.
- Trance: 0.4355 s against 0.4348 s.
- Acid house 0.4806 s, EDM 0.4691 s, Afro house 0.5007 s: the beat lengths at 125, 128 and 120 bpm.

The same capture with the next onset left out gives 0.117 s (Tropical house), 0.158 s (Trance) and 0.293 s (Dubstep), against 0.5562 s, 0.4355 s and 1.5004 s in the fixture. A tail made two or three times longer would still pass, so the Done-when line on length has no test.

Fix:
- In `measure`, end each segment before the next onset: `hi = int((nxt - t0) / span) - 1`, a guard block, as `one_hit` already slices.
- Re-record `tools/fixtures/kick_other_vibes.json` from a worktree of `d6052fd` with the fixed measure, with `recorded_from` stating it.
- Add a check that fails when any hit's length is within two blocks of its gap: the measure saturated.

### Should-fix

**S1. The sweep starts outside spec 10.5's 150 to 200 Hz in 12 of the 14 top vibes.** `src/music/kick.ts:44`: the sweep is a ratio of the body (2x to 7x), so where it starts depends on the key as much as on Punch. At each style's own key, only two top vibes start inside the band: Deep techno (164 Hz) and Deep house (186 Hz). The others start at:
- 216 to 289 Hz: Melodic 275, Synthwave 289, Progressive 261, Drone 257, 2-step 245, DnB 240, Lo-fi 239, Minimal 233, Dub 229, Downtempo 220, Breakbeat 216.
- 124 Hz: Ambient.

F1 rolls the key per launch, so the start also moves with each launch.

Fix:
- With Root on, set the sweep's start in hertz from Punch, so that the vibes' Punch values land in 150 to 200 Hz in any key. For example, start = 120 x (260/120)^Punch puts 150 to 200 Hz at Punch 0.3 to 0.65. Keep the 4.5x ratio for Root off at the default, so the ten other vibes stay identical.
- Add a unit test: every top vibe, in all twelve keys, starts in 150 to 200 Hz.

**S2. Two controls named Punch on the Simple Drums tab do different things, and they share a value.** The macro dial Punch (`src/music/macros.ts:70-78`, spec 8.3) moves drive, kickDecay and pitch. The quadrant's Punch axis (spec 10.5) moves kickPunch. The macro also moves kickDecay, which is the quadrant's Length axis, so turning the Punch dial moves the quadrant's dot sideways. With Root on, which all fourteen top vibes have, the macro's pitch target is dead.

Fix (one of these, or ask Pedro):
- Point the drums' Punch macro at `kickPunch` and drive, in place of kickDecay and pitch, so the dial and the quadrant's up axis are one idea.
- Or rename the quadrant axis.

Add a test that no Simple Drums macro targets a value the quadrant owns.

**S3. The `kick` part's sweep check fails when a kick reaches the audio clock late, and blames the kick voice for it.** This is the builder's single failure.

What happens:
- Without load the engine hands each kick to Web Audio 44 to 61 ms ahead: Tone's lookAhead of 0.1 s minus its 50 ms tick.
- I ran the sweep 18 times alone: no failure, every onset within 0.3 to 2.7 ms of its logged time. Once, a still stretch showed an envelope rise of 0.398 with kicks only 21 ms ahead, and the run still passed (see the third point under Weak).
- With a second browser running, 6 of 8 runs failed. Kicks were handed over 8 to 207 ms after their time, and their onsets in the capture came 10 to 184 ms after the logged time.
- Each late onset then reads as "a step in a sounding kick" and "a sounding kick rose by 0.8 to 1.0 (a retrigger)". The check looks for steps and rises from 35 to 50 ms after the logged time, not after the real onset.

Diagnosis: a main-thread stall makes the kick late. The kick voice does not click or retrigger, and the stall is not caused by F2's code: the same margin holds for every voice (M9).

The check is weak in three ways:
- (a) `late` (line 324) compares with `ctx.currentTime`, which trails the audio thread's render position. Onsets 10 to 43 ms late were seen with no "scheduled late" report.
- (b) Steps and rises are keyed to the logged time, not to the onset found in the capture.
- (c) The still stretch's rise is never judged, and a large one raises the swept threshold through `CLICK_FACTOR * rr` (line 515), which hides a defect in both stretches.

Fix, with no threshold loosened:
- Find each hit's onset in the capture: the first sample over 0.3 of the hit's peak from 2 ms before the logged time.
- Fail a hit whose onset is more than 3 ms after its logged time, with its own message: "kick N started X ms late (main thread)".
- Measure steps and rises from the found onset.
- Judge the still rise against `STILL_RISE`, as the still jump is judged against `STILL_JUMP_MAX` (line 511).
- The smoke runs one part at a time with no other browser. Say in the part's docstring that the sweep needs a quiet machine.

**S4. On the desk, the drums' filter pad is behind a sheet.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx:401-403`. Spec 9.2 says Advanced panels on the desk "show everything at once, with no pages". The Drum sound sheet now hides the drums' Tone and Edge (cutoff and resonance) behind a button.

Fix: put the filter pad beside the kick quadrant at the foot of the column, two pads of about 74 x 106 px in the 150 px column (both over 48 px). Or give the filter a row in the column's free height at 1280 x 800. Keep the pad out of the sheet, and update `kick.test.ts` and the shots' CONTROL_KEYS mapping (`xy=drums` → "drum sound").

**S5. Untested engine logic.**
- Nothing tests that `setPatch` keeps Punch, Click and Root (`src/engine/index.ts:735`).
- Nothing tests that `Drums.apply` (`src/engine/audio.ts:658-661`) puts a vibe without kick settings back on `KICK_DEFAULTS` after a top vibe. Going from Deep techno to Trance must give the membrane kick again.

Fix: two engine tests on the recorder mock or the Engine with a fake graph. One asserts that `kickState().settings` is unchanged after `setPatch("drums", "boom")`. The other asserts it equals `KICK_DEFAULTS` after loading a song of one of the ten other vibes over a top vibe.

### Minor

**M1. The tail's range differs from spec 10.5.** Length is the old kickDecay dial, so the decay runs from 0.071 to 0.566 s (`src/music/sound.ts:29`), against the spec's 0.15 to 0.5. It is also capped by the gap: at 122 bpm Four floor, Length 1 rings 0.243 s to -30 dB. The ten other vibes need the old curve to stay identical, so this is a fair choice. Fix: name it in the row's Choices with the numbers, and put the tail under Listen for.

**M2. With Root on, seven of twelve keys sit outside 45 to 60 Hz.** `src/music/kick.ts:50`. The band is under an octave, so seven keys cannot have an octave in it:
- C 65.4 Hz, C# 69.3, D 73.4, D# 38.9, E 41.2, F 43.7, B 61.7.
- D and C# sit nearly a fifth above the band.

The Done-when's "or its octave" assumes an octave exists. Fix: name it in Choices, with D (Lo-fi, Drone) and F (Deep techno, Deep house, DnB) under Listen for. Pedro's ear decides whether D should drop to 36.7 Hz.

**M3. The measured body is 10 to 14 cents flat in every key** (A 54.64 Hz where the voice sets 55.00). This is inside the 30-cent line but uses half of it, and I found no cause. Fix: `check_tune` could print a reading taken before the drive as well. If the offset is in Tone's ramp, say so in `kick.ts`.

**M4. `LENGTH_TOL_S = 0.010` (`tools/kickcheck.py:47`) is the builder's own.** The row asks for "the smoke's existing tolerance", and the smoke had none for length. The level tolerance reuses `dropcheck.LOUD_DB`, a loudness tolerance. Fix: say both in Choices.

**M5. Deep techno keeps Dub in its Groove and Break** (`src/styles/deepTechno.ts:63, 73`), where the table says "Thump, ... sparse hats". Dub is a kick on every beat with offbeat hats, which is close. F4 covers only the other ten top vibes, so no later row will revisit Deep techno's beat. Fix: say it in Choices, or move Groove and Break to Thump with a sparse hat added in the section's grid.

**M6. Two names for one value, and a dead dial.** On the Advanced Kick page, "K decay" and the quadrant's "Length" are the same value. With Root on, the Tune dial still turns and does nothing. Fix: label the dial "Length". Dim Tune when Root is on, with Root's sub-label saying so.

**M7. On a 412 x 915 phone and the tablet paged layouts, the drums' filter pad sits lowest, under the kick quadrant** (`InstrumentTab.tsx:539`). The control touched most should be lowest (8.6). Fix: on the Kick page, show the quadrant in the filter pad's place, or order the quadrant after the filter panel.

**M8. On the desk's 150 px quadrant (1280 x 800 Advanced), the dot at 50 · 50 covers the start of the readout.** Fix: inset the readout by the dot's radius on pads under 200 px, or let the dot pass under it.

**M9. The scheduling margin is thin, for every voice (pre-existing).** Notes reach the audio clock 44 to 61 ms before their time. A main-thread stall longer than that makes them late. Fix: add a Carried item to weigh a larger `lookAhead`, for example 0.15 s, against the latency of live taps.

**M10. Listen for at the close.** Include:
- The drum filter's 18 kHz top against 10 kHz elsewhere (C2), as the row asks.
- The click, band-passed white noise into the drive, which is a new treble source next to C1's crackle.

## Verdict

**fix**: 1 error, 5 should-fix, 10 minor.
