## Risk 1: the oscillator-leak patch (`src/engine/audio.ts` 46-75)

- **The diagnosis is right and the fix is complete.** Tone's `disconnect()` (`ToneAudioNode.js`) resolves a ToneAudioNode or an AudioParam. A Tone `Param` is neither, so the old patch ran a bare `disconnect()` on the frequency and detune signals' native output. That cut those signals from every oscillator the instrument had, including the one playing. `connectSignal` had set each native param to 0, so the playing note fell to 0 Hz.
- **I reproduced it outside the app** with a Tone.Synth: a 0.1 s note, then a second note 5 ms after the first one's release ends. The pitch of the second note:
  - with the old patch, 0 Hz in 12 of 12 runs (sawtooth, fatsawtooth, fmsine, square);
  - with the new patch and with no patch, the right pitch in 12 of 12.
- **It covers every oscillator.** Fat, FM, AM, Pulse and PWM oscillators and Tone's LFO all start through `Oscillator._start`, so the one patch covers them. Noise has no frequency signal. The native oscillators in `mods.ts` and `fx.ts` are not affected.
- **The glides and the drift pitch route were cut too, and are restored.** The Swoop and lead glides (`audio.ts` 1223, 1275) write to the voice's frequency signal, and the drift pitch route (989) goes into its detune signal. The old patch cut both from the oscillators.
- **The leak still stops.** Chrome throws InvalidAccessError when a connection that does not exist is disconnected (checked: the first disconnect worked, the second threw). With the new patch no disconnect threw in my probe, so each one removed a real connection, and the ended oscillator is no longer reachable.
- **The earlier checks were blind to it.** At 0 Hz an oscillator holds the DC value of its waveform at phase 0: peaks of 0.11 to 0.85 in my probe, not silence. A peak meter above -60 dB reads that as sound. It read as silence only when the held value was near 0, which is why the bass showed as silent in only about half of the walks.
- **Every row since G5 (23 Nov) was affected.** Any Tone voice whose next start was scheduled before the previous oscillator's ended event fired lost its pitch. That covers kick and perc, bass, synth stabs, arps and leads in every row since. A held DC gated by a fast envelope is a step, so these listen items were heard on a broken engine: C1 (crackle), C11 (Drift sparse) and C13 (unexplained jumps of 0.43 of the peak on a Drift sine; this is a likely cause). See S3.

## Risk 2: load

- **The per-vibe figures carry the previous vibe's tails.** `tools/cost.py vibes` loads the vibes one after another on one page, in picker order. I measured on a fresh page per vibe (median of 3, 3 s after landing, 6 s traced), with the effect slots off, and with the transport stopped:

  | Vibe | Load | Slots off | Stopped |

  | Trance | 71.3% | 70.1% | 41.7% |

- **Drone.** Drone's 73 to 78% comes from Ambient's 8 to 20 s releases still ringing when Drone loads, because Drone follows Ambient in picker order. - **Trance is the real heaviest.** The effects cost about 1 point. The load is voices: 12 supersaw synth voices of two oscillators each, from Gate's 10 hits a bar with a 0.5 s release, plus 9 string voices. Future garage holds up to 15 synth voices.
- **Judged against this box's own reference scenes, every vibe is acceptable.** On this box the slots-off scene measured 77.4% and the six-slot scene 82.1%. Pedro accepted the six-slot scene (spec 8.8). Every vibe's median is under it; Trance (77.6 to 81.2%) comes closest.
- **The phone is not measured.** Absolute figures from this x86 container do not transfer to a Snapdragon 8 Elite. That is a phone measurement for G6 and a listen item: Trance, and Ambient into Drone, are the places to listen for crackle.
- **I found no cheaper build that keeps the sound exactly.** The cost is the voice count, not the effects. The nearest change is in M5.

## Errors

**E1. C10 is not done by choice for Drone and Ambient, and their cards break spec 8.10's long chords.** The files are `src/data/progressions/*.ts` (the `vibes` tags) and `src/music/progressions.test.ts` 29-49.
- Drone is tagged with 40 cards. 34 of them change chord every 1 or 2 bars, such as Sunny side, Morning run, Victory lap and Fly high, all bright or uplifting at 1 bar a chord.
- Ambient is tagged with 42 cards and 36 of them do the same; Balloon changes every half bar.
- `SimpleChords.tsx` 98 applies a card's lengths exactly as written. So in Simple, a card picked in Drone at 60 bpm changes chord every 4 seconds. Spec 8.10 says Ambient and Drone have chords of 4 or 8 bars, and that only the other vibes keep half a bar to 2.
- Only about six library cards have chords of 4 or 8 bars, so the "at least seven per feel" quota could not be met with fitting cards. It was filled with cards that do not fit.

Fix:
1. Add progression files with chords of 4 and 8 bars, tagged `ambient` and `drone`, until each of the six feels has seven or more for both vibes.
2. Remove the 1-bar and 2-bar cards from those two vibes.
3. Add a test: every card tagged `ambient` or `drone` has every chord of 4 bars or more.

## Should-fix

**S1. The oscillator fix has no regression test, and its `catch` hides failure.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 62-74.
- Nothing fails if the patch goes back to the bare disconnect, or if a Tone upgrade changes how `_param` resolves. In the second case the `catch {}` swallows the throw and the leak comes back with no sign.
- Fix: add a smoke check built like my probe. A Synth plays a short note and a second one 5 ms after the first one's release ends, and the check reads the pitch of the second (it failed 12 of 12 with the old patch). Then count the throws the `catch` swallows on a debug counter and require 0 after a walk.

**S2. The per-vibe "every instrument audible" check would not reliably have caught the silent bass.** `tools/smoke.py` 1914-1921 (`solo_bar`, peak above -60 dB over about a bar).
- A frozen 0 Hz oscillator holds a DC level, and the meter reads that as sound.
- Notes lost here and there within a bar pass.
- The check fails only when a whole bar is silent, which is why the bug showed in about half of the walks.
- Fix: read the instrument meter through a DC-blocking high-pass at 20 Hz, and over two bars count the onsets on the bass (or require level on at least most of its scheduled hits), not one peak.

**S3. Carried items must say that the listen notes before P8 were heard on an engine that dropped pitch.** `docs/BACKLOG.md` Carried. Add a carried item:
- from G5 to P9, short notes on every Tone voice could fall to 0 Hz and hold a DC step (fixed in P8, 10e7d6f);
- re-listen to C1, C11 and C13 on the P8 build;
- the builder re-runs C13's 0.43 jump measurement on Drift.

**S4. Drone's groove, the section it opens on, changes chord every 4 bars, as Ambient's does.** `src/styles/drone.ts` 49 (`[[0,4],[1,4],[0,4],[6,4]]`), `README.md` 31 ("Drone holds one chord for eight bars").
- Spec 8.10 says Drone is the one that "stays on one note or chord for a long time". As built it stays no longer than Ambient (`ambient.ts` 51).
- Drone also shares Ambient's drums, bass, synth patch and rhythms (soft, sub, padSwell, pedal, sustained); see M6.
- Fix: give the groove chords of 8 bars (for example `[[0,8],[6,8]]`, taken from or added to the library and tagged `drone`). Keep the README line true.

**S5. Some tag sets read as a quota fill, not a choice.** `src/data/progressions/*.ts`, test at `progressions.test.ts` 36-49.
- Every vibe has exactly 8 cards per feel, or 9 in one feel.
- Melodic techno and Trap share 38 cards, a Jaccard overlap of 0.70 and the highest pair. Trap carries warm house-mood cards (Low lamp, Soft rain, Blanket, Corner shop).
- The test allows an overlap of up to 0.8, so it would pass the blanket groups C10 set out to remove.
- Fix: re-pick Trap's and Melodic techno's lists, then lower the limit to 0.6.

**S6. Trance is close to a copy of Progressive.** `src/styles/trance.ts` 9-26 against `src/styles/progressive.ts` 9-26.
- The two share: A minor; pluckLead in arp mode on Layer 1; strings on Layer 2; the rolling bass; and the same effect slots (synth Echo on with Chorus, Layer 1 Echo on with Flanger, Layer 2 Phaser on with Chorus).
- Their beats differ only by Trance's shaker row. The `progressive` beat places its hits where Four floor does; only the velocities differ.
- Fix: give Trance its own identity. For example: another key, the offbeat bass, a trance gate on the pad through the Gate effect, or a supersaw lead on Layer 2, and a different set of effects. Change the `progressive` beat so it differs from Four floor in its hits.

**S7. `tools/cost.py vibes` measures each vibe with the previous vibe's tails.** `tools/cost.py` 55-65.
- Drone reads 73 to 78% in the tool and about 50% fresh.
- Fix: measure each vibe on a fresh page, or stop and wait 20 s before loading it. Report the Ambient-to-Drone transition as its own figure. Correct the builder's Drone 78% in the changelog line.

## Minor

**M1. The Acid beat is the Techno beat plus a clap on beat 2.** `src/data/beats/acid.ts`. The hats, open hats and kick sit where Techno's do, a hit distance of 1. Fix: give Acid its own shape, for example 16th hats with off-step accents and a ghost perc or shaker.

**M2. The frame walk counts the chip pages it turns but never asserts the count.** `tools/shots.py` 159, 189. If `data-more` were renamed, "every name fits" would check first pages only and still pass. Fix: assert `w.chips` is at least the sum of the pages of the paged pickers (the beat picker alone has 7).

**M3. The borrow picker's 24 tiles are not checked for 48 px.** `tools/smoke.py` 1762-1790 checks the vibe picker only. Fix: run the `PICKER` height check on the `#chord-vibe` picker at 360 x 640.

**M4. Counts in the commit message are wrong.** Commit e2c58cb says "25 beats" and "9 to 18 patches". There are 26 beats and 9 to 17 patches. The changelog line should use 26 and 9 to 17.

**M5. Trance's cost.** `src/styles/trance.ts` 11. The supersaw's 0.5 s release under Gate's 10 hits a bar keeps about 12 voices of two oscillators each sounding. Setting the release to about 0.2 s in Trance's patch set would roughly halve the overlap. That changes the tails slightly, so it is Pedro's call; measure it fresh.

**M6. Drone and Ambient share their drums, bass, synth patch and rhythms.** `src/styles/drone.ts` 16-18. Drone's difference rests on its mood, its pads and its envelopes. Consider a darker synth patch for Drone. Pedro's ear.
