## Findings

### Errors

**E1. A section name the app accepts does not fit its strip tile.** `src/ui/App.tsx:84` (`<span>{view.sub}</span>`), `src/engine/song.ts:200` (`MAX_SECTION_NAME = 12`), `src/style.css:256`.
Reproduced at 360 x 640 on the built app: a section renamed `Breakdown 2` (11 characters) overflows the strip tile on the Advanced Play tab (text 64 px in a 59 px tile, after the fit has already shrunk it to 10 px); a 12-character name overflows in every mode (103 px in 80 px). Spec 8.6: text never overflows, and "a label that still does not fit at 10 px is shortened in the source". User text cannot be shortened in the source, so the source must make it fit.
Fix: make the strip's word under the letter always fit at 10 px in the narrowest tile (59 px, Advanced Play at 360). Either lower `MAX_SECTION_NAME` to what fits there with wide glyphs (measure it; about 8), or keep the name length and give the strip a pure, tested `stripWord(name, maxChars)` that shortens it (the full name stays in the section sheet's title). Apply the same rule to any other place a section or song name sits in a tile.

**E2. The "(tested)" text-fit line has no test that fails for the case that breaks it.** `tools/shots.py` (`walk_frame`), `tools/smoke.py:1041`.
The walk only ever sees the default names (Intro, Groove, Break, Outro) and default songs, so E1 passes `smoke` and `shots.py`. Per RUN.md 4c this is an error.
Fix: in `walk_frame`, before walking, rename one section and the song to the longest names the inputs accept, written in wide letters (`"W" * MAX_SECTION_NAME`, `"W" * MAX_SONG_NAME`), walk every screen including both sheets, then restore the names. The walk must fail on today's build and pass after E1.

### Should-fix

**S1. Text fit does a forced layout over every control on every change.** `src/ui/fit.ts:50` and `:72`.
`fit()` calls `leaves(c)` (a walk with `getClientRects()` on every descendant) before it checks its cache, and `startFit` runs `fitAll` on every DOM mutation. While playing, the bar counter and beat lights change on every step and the Mix meter about 15 times a second, so each frame re-walks and lays out every button on screen on the main thread, which also runs Tone's lookahead scheduler. Spec 4 (phone CPU is the budget) and 8.12 (audio comes first).
Fix: key the cache on the control's own `textContent` and size (read once, no tree walk) and only call `leaves()` when that key changes; or observe only `childList`/`characterData` under controls whose text can change and skip the transport's own readouts (fixed-width, tabular figures, sized in CSS).

**S2. The Now tile reads the lagging section.** `src/ui/tabs/PlayTab.tsx:23` and `:29`.
The tile shows `song.sections[playing].on[id]` and its pending state from `playing = pos.section`, but the tap calls `toggleInstrumentInPlaying`, which uses the scheduler's section. Right after a section switch lands, for up to the lookahead, a tap toggles the new section while the tile still draws the old one, and the pending mark looks for a toggle in the wrong section, so the tap shows no "Next bar".
Fix: expose the scheduler's section from the engine (or have `pendingShown()` include it) and use the same index for reading and toggling; add a unit test or a smoke step that taps a Now tile within one lookahead of a switch landing.

**S3. Chip rows lost their names.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx:221` (Rhythm page: rhythm chips then wave chips with no heading), `src/ui/tabs/LayerTab.tsx:91` (Arp: direction, rate and octaves rows unlabelled), `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx` `Waves` and `DrumBeat` (the "Wave" and "Sequencer" labels removed).
The labels were cut to make the pages fit. "1/16 1/8 1/4" and "Saw Square Tri Sine" read as unnamed rows; spec 8.12 lists group headings as a place for icons, so headings are expected.
Fix: put back one short group heading per chip row (11 px, one line, the 8.12 small-label style), or fold the name into the chip row as a non-interactive first cell; re-run the walk to confirm the pages still fit at 360 x 640.

**S4. On a short screen the Layer tab no longer says which layer or section it edits.** `src/ui/tabs/LayerTab.tsx:192`.
At 360 x 640 `HeadRow` is dropped, so "Layer 1 · B Groove" and "on at the next bar" are gone. With Edit without playing active (Advanced) the user edits a section that is not playing with nothing on the tab saying which; the Drums, Bass and Synth tabs keep their head row at the same size.
Fix: keep a one-line head (name and section letter, 12 px) on the layer tab, for instance inside the mode row beside the toggle, or show the edited section letter on the page chip as the Chords tab does (`Chords · B`).

### Minor

**M1. New jam is disabled while playing.** `src/ui/App.tsx:279`. Spec 8.2: New jam is "always one tap away", and a vibe loaded while playing lands on the next bar line. G5's keeper cannot switch songs while running, so this is the honest state today. Fix: add a line to Carried for P3 (New jam while playing lands on the next bar line), and record the choice in the changelog.

**M2. The mode switch went into the header instead of the row's temporary Play-tab switch, and is not remembered.** `src/ui/App.tsx:196`. Reasonable (the row also asks for the mode switch in the header), but it is not stored, so every launch starts in Simple even after choosing Advanced (spec 8.1 says it is remembered; P2's job). Fix: record the choice under Choices in the changelog; either persist it now in the keeper's settings or name it in P2's row as still to do.

**M3. The XY readout sits under the dot.** `src/ui/components.tsx:199`. With the pad now sized by flex, the dot at high cutoff covers "10.0 kHz · 0%" (360 x 640, Drums Filter page) and "4.0 kHz · 20%" (412 x 915, Layer 1). Fix: place the readout in the top-right corner, or draw it above the dot (z-index) on a dark backing.

**M4. `plays()` is untested logic in a component.** `src/ui/tabs/PlayTab.tsx:13`. Fix: move it to `src/ui/logic.ts` beside `stripState` and add a unit test (preset, edited preset, rhythm, layer mode).

**M5. Dead space above the Advanced Play grid at 412 x 915.** `src/style.css` `.grid { flex: 0 1 384px }`. About 280 px of empty panel above the grid. Not a rule break (the grid sits lowest, in thumb reach), but the cap could grow to fill, keeping rows at most about 96 px. Fix: raise the flex basis or let rows grow with `minmax(48px, 1fr)` and no cap.
