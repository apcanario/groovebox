## Findings

### Errors

**E1. At 1024 x 768 no Advanced panel is whole; every one keeps its pages.** Spec 9.1: "Advanced uses pages only where a panel cannot fit at 1024 x 768"; row T2: "every knob of an instrument visible at once on tablet landscape". 1024 x 768 is the size the spec names as the threshold, and there all seven panels page (probe; tab body 426 x 632). This is by construction, not by measurement: `src/style.css` line 2819 (`.mixtab.whole { min-width: 600px }`), line 2824 (`.chordtab.whole { min-width: 600px }`) and line 2846 (`.insttab.drumwhole { min-width: 640px }`) force an overflow under those widths, and the stacked form of Bass, Synth and the layers (`@container (max-width: 619.98px)`, line 2699) is taller than 632 px. The 1024 x 768 Mix Levels screenshot shows half the panel empty. Nothing guards the size: `tools/shots.py` line 711 `PAGELESS` names only 1280 x 800 and 800 x 1280. The Done-when line only names 1280 x 800, so it is true, but the row's first and third bullets are not met. Fix: give the desk at 1024 x 768 in Advanced a wider instrument half (the `@media (min-width: 1200px)` split at line 2619 extended down to 1000 px, or about 50/50: the pads half at 1024 x 768 has room, its pads are about 110 px wide), and a one-column whole form of Mix (Master and Space dials, Levels as faders, Drop and Morph under) and of Chords (Key, Chords, then the library) that does not set a 600 px floor; then add `(1024, 768)` to `PAGELESS` with every panel that fits, and list in this report's successor each panel that still cannot, with the measured overflow. If a panel truly cannot fit at 1024 x 768 at 48 px, say so with the numbers rather than by a CSS `min-width`.

**E2. The whole/paged fallback flips mid-jam and is not deterministic: the same screen shows whole or paged depending on history.** `src/ui/tabs/full.ts` lines 23 to 41. The fallback is remembered per body size and shape only, and it never comes back while the tab stays open. Probed at 768 x 1024 Advanced Chords: whole with up to 4 chords; adding the 5th chord switches the panel to pages under the finger (and `ChordEditor` remounts in another tree position, so the chord selection is lost); going back to 2 chords stays paged; leaving the tab and coming back shows it whole again. The same happens without a tap when the edited section follows the playing one into a section with more chords, which is a layout shift in the middle of a jam. Fix: decide whole or paged from inputs that do not change while playing: measure the panel at its worst case for its shape (the chord sequence at `MAX_CHORDS`, the Audio note line present, the longest section name; for example render the whole panel once off-screen or with a `data-worst` flag), or reserve the chord sequence's two rows in the whole Chords so its height does not depend on the chord count; key the decision on body size and shape only, and add a smoke check that at 768 x 1024 and 800 x 1280 the Chords panel's whole/paged state is the same with 1 chord and with 8.

### Should-fix

**S1. Mix at 800 x 1280 fits only by the 1 px rounding allowance.** `src/ui/tabs/full.ts` line 12 (`+ 1`) with `src/style.css` `.fader .ftrack { min-height: var(--hit) }`. Probe: body `scrollHeight` 575 against `clientHeight` 574, section 567 against 566, fader tracks at their 48 px floor (zero slack). It already overflows by a pixel and a font metric, a zoom step or a longer Audio note pushes it to pages. Fix: free at least 8 px in the whole Mix over the dock (the always-there `data-audio-state` line can share the Audio note's line, or the Motion note goes to the pages, as the Space note did), and add to the walk a minimum-slack check for the panels `PAGELESS` names (fader track or XY pad at least 8 px above its floor).

**S2. The fader meters add a second animation loop and re-render the whole Mix panel 15 times a second.** `src/ui/tabs/MixTab.tsx` lines 191 to 205 (`useInstrumentMeters`). A second `requestAnimationFrame` loop beside `MasterMeter`'s (line 17); every 66 ms it allocates an entries array and an object (`Object.fromEntries`, line 199) and calls `setM`, which re-renders `WholeMix` with every dial, Drop and Morph, and each render runs `useFull`'s un-dependent layout effect (full.ts line 38), which reads `scrollHeight` and forces a layout. Spec 9.5: one animation loop, audio first. Fix: one loop for both meters (MasterMeter's, or the motion loop of `src/ui/motion.ts`), writing each fader's meter straight to its `.fmeter` element's `transform` (or a `--m` custom property on `.faders`) through a ref, with no component state and no allocation per tick; `Fader` then takes no `meter` prop. Note: the meters keep moving under Still, as the master meter always has; whether a meter is "movement" is T3's call (spec 9.5), recorded here only.

**S3. "No knob lost" is tested for Drums' knob lists only, not for what the panels draw.** `src/ui/tabs/full.test.ts` line 29 checks that `VOICE_KNOBS` plus `DRUM_EXTRAS` equal the paged Drums knob lists; it would still pass if `WholeDrums` stopped rendering `DRUM_EXTRAS`, Swing, Reset, the beat presets or an effect slot. Bass, Synth, the layers, Mix and Chords have no parity check. By reading the code every control of each paged panel is in its whole form today (Drums: the 8-step view is a view of the same grid, the 16-step grid covers it; the sound picker moved into a sheet). Fix: in the tablet walk, for each panel in `PAGELESS`, collect the set of control keys of the whole panel (each dial's id, `data-step16`, `data-fxpick`, `data-hint`, `data-motion`, `data-mode`, `data-leadstyle`, `data-fader` against the paged Levels dials' ids, the send labels, the XY pad's id) and compare with the union over every page of the same panel at a paged size (1024 x 768 today); fail on any key missing.

### Minor

**M1. The whole Drums panel has no Drums lane.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx` line 412 (`WholeDrums`). Spec 8.12: "On every instrument tab the instrument's own lane sits above its controls"; Bass, Synth and the layers keep theirs in the head row. The grid's playing-step outline carries the playhead, so the loss is small. Fix: put the lane in `.dleft` over the beat chips if the 18 px of XY slack allows, or record the choice for Pedro.

**M2. A page chosen on a paged panel is lost when a rotation crosses whole and paged.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx` line 489 and `LayerTab.tsx` `PagedLayer`: the paged component remounts, so its `chosen` state resets (probe: Drums FX page chosen at 800 x 1280, to 1280 x 800 and back, shows Beat). T1's review recorded that page state survived rotation. No stuck state was found (the paged form always shows a real page, and the whole form returns on rotation back). Fix: lift `chosen` into the tab component (above the whole/paged switch) or key it by panel in a small module map.

**M3. A fader jumps to the touch point.** `src/ui/components.tsx` line 283: `pointerdown` sets the value from the touch position, so a finger landing on a fader can jump an instrument's level by up to 100 (an audible step), unlike the relative-drag dials. Fix: on `pointerdown` remember the value and the y, and move by the drag's delta (as `dragValue` does for dials); keep tap-to-set out.

**M4. Two rows of one voice look the same as two voices.** `src/style.css` line 2863 (`.drumwhole .drow`): all twelve rows sit 2 px apart, so Kick's second half reads as a row of its own until the "Decay" dial is read. Fix: a 10 px gap (`--group`) between voices, or a voice-coloured left edge on each pair; there is 18 px of XY slack at 1280 x 800, so a smaller step (4 px) may be what fits.

**M5. At 800 x 1280 a layer switches between whole and paged as the edited section changes mode.** Probe: Layer 1 flipped twice while the sections were played in turn (Lead in some sections, Arp, Pad or Drone in others). This follows from the shape (a lead does not fit over the dock) and is deterministic per section, but it is a layout change on a section change. Fix: none needed if Pedro accepts it; otherwise, at 800 x 1280 page a layer when any section of the song has it in Lead.

## Drums as two rows of eight

Spec 9.2: "Drums: every voice by 16 steps as one grid". As built, each voice's 16 steps are two rows of eight (steps 1 to 8 over 9 to 16), twelve rows in one grid, all on screen at once with the voice's level and decay at the end of each row, the playing step outlined. Sixteen 48 px cells across need 798 px; the panel is 649 px wide at 1280 x 800, so 16 across does not fit at 48 px. Judgment: it satisfies the spec's intent (every step of every voice editable at once, no pages, one grid) but not its shape (a voice is not one row of 16, and the playhead jumps rows mid-bar). Not a finding; a choice for Pedro's veto, with M4 to make the pairs read.
