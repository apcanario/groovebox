# G3 review: Drums, Bass, Synth and Mix tabs

Reviewer: Opus 5.5, fresh agent, had not seen the build. Base `d10bbde`, head `2009b4c`. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/SPEC.md`, row G3, Carried C1 and the G1 changelog entry.

## Scripts

- `npm run check`: **pass** (tsc, 103 tests in 4 files, vite build).
- `npm run smoke`: **fail, 3 runs out of 3.** Every other check passes (all tabs at 390 and 360 px, no control under 48 px, no screen over six dials, 2-step and DnB at 174 bpm audible with peak -7.7 dBFS, no attacks after Stop). The one failure is the same each time: `drum voice levels did not silence the drums (-17.3 -> -29.8 dB)`, then `-19.5 -> -38.2` and `-18.6 -> -28.7`.

Rhythm and drum-pattern changes act at once, which is a reasonable reading of section 3 (only section switches and on/off are bar-line). Ramps: S1 is the one stepped parameter.

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Drum voice levels do not reach zero; smoke fails.** `src/engine/audio.ts:323` (`this.gains[voice].gain.rampTo(...)`). With all four voice levels at 0 and the drums soloed, the master still reads -29 to -44 dB. Probe in headless Chrome (scratch script, no source change): after `setParam('drums', 'hatLevel' | 'openLevel', 0)` Tone's timeline for those gains says 0 (`gain.value === 0`, last event `linearRampToValueAtTime 0`), but the native `AudioParam.value` stays at about 0.14 (hat 0.145, open 0.136) and analysers on the hat and open gain outputs show signal (peak 0.04 to 0.08). Kick and clap gains reach 0 in most runs. A hard `cancelScheduledValues(0); setValueAtTime(0, 0)` through Tone did not clear it either; muting the voice synth's own `volume` did. So the hat and open level dials at 0 leave the voice sounding at about -17 dB below its default, which fails the Done-when for "per-voice level", and the row cannot be pushed with smoke failing. Fix: put each voice's level on the voice synth's own `volume` (`this.kick/hat/open/clap.volume.rampTo(dB, RAMP)`, with a floor such as -80 dB at level 0, or `mute` at exactly 0 once the ramp has finished), or keep the gain but drive it with a native `cancelAndHoldAtTime(now)` + `linearRampToValueAtTime`, and verify the native `AudioParam.value` reaches the target. Then make smoke check each voice alone (each `<voice>Level` at 0 with the other three at default drops that voice's contribution), not only all four together (`tools/smoke.py:307-314`), and run smoke three times in a row to show it is not flaky.

### Should-fix

**S1. Synth detune is stepped, not ramped.** `src/engine/audio.ts:460-464`. `detune` calls `PolySynth.set({ oscillator: { spread } })`, which sets each fat oscillator's detune at once, so dragging the dial on a held chord (synth rhythm Held) jumps the beating in steps. It does not click, but section 7 says parameter changes are ramped, never stepped, and this is the one continuous dial that is not. Fix: for every active voice of both voice sets, ramp each sub-oscillator's `detune` to its new spread position over `RAMP` (Tone's fat oscillator spreads `count` oscillators evenly over `spread` cents), and store `this.spread` for new voices as now. Add the ramp to the smoke walk by turning Detune while the synth is on Held.

**S2. Kick decay can run past the next kick.** `src/music/sound.ts:20` (`kick: 1.25` beats) and `src/engine/g3.test.ts:58`. The comment says the cap means "a tail never swallows the next hit at 170 to 176 bpm", but at decay 1 the kick lasts `min(0.2 x 2.83, 1.25 beats)`: 0.57 s at 118 bpm against a 0.51 s beat in four on the floor, 0.43 s at 174 bpm against a 0.34 s beat, and in 2-step the kicks on steps 7 and 10 are only three sixteenths apart. The mono MembraneSynth then restarts its pitch sweep while the old tail still sounds. The test only checks the default decay of 0.5, so it passes. Fix: cap each voice at the shortest gap of the playing pattern (or at 1 beat for the kick and accept 2-step overlap as a recorded choice), and extend the test to decay 1 at every tempo in `TEMPOS`.

### Minor

**M1. Tempo test covers only the default decay.** `src/engine/g3.test.ts:58-71`. See S2; also loop `amount` over `[0, 0.5, 1]` so the 170 to 176 bpm rule is tested over the dial's whole range.

**M2. Drum and sub levels go above the C1 trims.** `src/music/sound.ts:29` (`drumLevel` up to 1.33x) and `:40` (`subGain` up to 1.2, default 0.9). At the top of the dial the hat and clap sit 2.5 dB above the trims C1 lowered for the crackle, and the sub 2.5 dB above its old level. Fix: cap `drumLevel` at 1 for `hat` and `open` (or for all voices) so the dial only lowers from the C1 trim, or record in the changelog that the dial can exceed it and tell Pedro to listen for crackle with hats at full.

**M3. The bass lost its second filter.** `src/engine/audio.ts:395`. The G1 bass was a `MonoSynth` with its own -24 dB/oct filter plus the channel filter; now it is a plain `Synth` through the channel filter only, so a saw bass with the filter envelope open carries more upper harmonics into the drive than before C1 was set. The 6 kHz top stays. Fix: none needed in code if the choice is recorded; list it under Choices in the changelog and add "saw bass with Env amt high, listen for crackle" to the Listen-for lines.

**M4. Layer square detune changed from 14 to 22 cents.** `src/engine/audio.ts:440` and `:169-171`. `oscFor` used 22 cents for saw and 14 for square; now every Poly uses `this.spread` (0.37 x 60 = 22 cents) for both, and layer patches get `detune` applied through `apply()`. Layer 1 and Layer 2 on square sound wider than in G2, outside this row's scope. Fix: keep 14 cents for square on the layers (pass the old per-wave spread when `fatAll` is false), or record the change.

**M5. Re-lighting a sequencer step moves an offbeat hit.** `src/music/presets.ts:126-134`. A lit step clears both sixteenths; an unlit one sets the even sixteenth. In 4/4, Shuffle and 2-step the hats sit on odd sixteenths, so tapping a hat step off and on again moves it from the "e"/"a" to the eighth and changes the groove. Fix: when lighting step `i`, use the sixteenth the section's preset had at `2i` or `2i+1` (first one with a hit), else `2i`; add a test that off then on restores the preset row.

**M6. Patch schema carries every instrument's keys on every patch.** `src/engine/song.ts:209-221`. Drums store `envAmount`, `detune`, `sub`, `glide`; bass and synth store eight drum keys; layers store all of them. Harmless now, but the song JSON of G5 will carry them. Fix: none required; note in the changelog that unused keys are kept for a flat schema, or drop them per instrument in `defaultPatch` and `validateSong`.

**M7. Knobs that act from the next note need a changelog line.** `src/engine/audio.ts:321`, `:324`, `:387`. Kick pitch, drum decays and bass glide act from the next hit, not at once (spec section 3: knobs act at once). This is the reasonable reading for per-hit parameters and cannot click, but it is a choice. Fix: record it under Choices in the G3 changelog entry.

**M8. Restart release path untested in the engine.** `src/engine/index.ts` (`for (const id of r.restarted) ...releaseAll(time)`). The sequencer's `restarted` is unit-tested; the engine releasing the held bass or synth before the restarted note is not, and smoke changes rhythms without checking for a held note. Fix: in the smoke walk, set the synth to Held, switch it to Stabs mid-chord, and check the synth's active voice count (`polys.live.synth.activeVoices`) drops within a bar.

**M9. Drum filter still tops out at 18 kHz.** `src/engine/audio.ts:276` (`[200, 18000]`). Not changed by G3 and not undone, but C1 says filters top out at 10 kHz and the Drums tab now puts that XY pad in front of Pedro. Fix: carry it to Pedro's C1 listen; if crackle persists, lower to 12 kHz (above the 7.5 kHz hat high-pass).

## Verdict

**fix.** 1 error, 2 should-fix, 9 minor. The tabs, presets, sequencer, pages and meter are built as the row asks and the C1 settings are intact; the row fails its Done-when only because the drum voice level gains do not reach zero in Chrome, which makes `smoke` fail every run.
