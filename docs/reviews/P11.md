## The smoke check changes (asked by the main chat)

**Hands-win read (5112945, af42369): a fixed measuring error, not a weakened check.** Morph's hold counts bar lines: a hand at clock T holds bar lines T+1 to T+16, which plan journey bars b0 to b0+15; bar b0+16 is the first Morph may move again (`morphHeld`, `index.ts`, `this.morphClock - t <= HANDS_BARS`). The old check waited `HANDS_BARS + 1` bars from `hand_bar` and so read the dial after bar b0+16 had been planned, where a move is correct. The new one reads while `morphBar() == b0 + 16` (bars b0..b0+15 planned, b0+16 not), checks it read at exactly that bar (and fails loudly if not), and keeps the move-list check over `[hand_bar, b0+16)`. Tested for real on the live build with a harness that runs the same block (`/tmp/p11rev/hands.py`, imports `morphcheck.py`):

- As built: passes. b0 = 7, dial 0.0998 by hand, unchanged through bar 22, Morph's first move on it at bar 23 = b0+16.
- `morphHeld` forced false: fails, with Morph's moves at bars 7, 8, 9 and the value 0.0998 -> 0.26.
- Hold broken only from bar b0+15 (the last bar inside the 16): fails, Morph's move at bar 22 and 0.0998 -> 0.11.

So a Morph move on any bar inside the 16, the last included, still fails the check.

**Pad page dial count 5 -> 6:** correct, Voicing is the sixth dial; the loop that follows turns every dial and fails if one changes nothing, so Voicing is checked too.

**DROP width (dropcheck.py:104):** now DROP plus the Morph switch must fill the row (within 40 px) and DROP alone must be at least half the width. Spec 8.6 puts the Morph switch beside DROP, so the old full-width test could not hold; the new one still fails a narrow or missing DROP, and morphcheck.py:89 checks Morph sits on DROP's row to its right at 48 px or more. Not weakened beyond what the spec asks. (Spec 8.9 and 8.12 still say "full width"; 8.6 is the later, specific line. See M4.)

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. An instrument Morph took out, or a Lock it eased, is stranded whenever the journey restarts or stops: Stop then Play, a song loaded, Morph switched off, a seed change.** `src/engine/index.ts:440` (`play()` sets `morphPlanner = null`), `:396` (load), `:1537` (a new planner when the seed changes, e.g. a song without a vibe renamed), `:1540` (Morph off only passes bars); the memory of what to give back lives only in the planner (`src/music/morph.ts:253-254`, `ease` and `out`). Probe on Ambient (dry engine, the scheduler's path): Morph takes the drums out of section B at bar 80; with a Stop and Play at bar 81 the drums are still off 160 bars later and no move ever brings them back; with Morph switched off at bar 81 they are still off 40 bars later. The song document keeps it, so autosave and Songs keep a jam without its drums. Spec 8.11: instruments "drop out and return"; Lock eases "then holds again". Fix: before a journey is dropped or paused (Play, `loadSong`/`loadAtBar`, a new seed, Morph switched off, and see S1 for a drop), make the planner's restoring moves at once through the same calls (toggle the out instrument back on in its section, set each eased Lock back to its base), unless a hand holds that control; e.g. a `MorphPlanner.release(held)` returning those moves, called from those paths. Add a dry-engine test for each path (Stop/Play, Morph off, load) that fails on the current code.

### Should-fix

**S1. A drop pressed while Morph has an instrument out plays the whole drop without it.** `src/engine/index.ts:1539-1540`: the drop holds all of Morph, including the instrument's return, so a drop pressed during a drop-out (Ambient may take the drums or bass out for a 16-bar loop) builds, drops and winds down with that instrument silent. Probe: drums out at bar 80, DROP at 81, drums off at every bar after. Spec 8.9: "The drop: every instrument of the section, plus extra layers." Fix: on the bar line the drop starts holding Morph, first make the restoring moves of E1 (instrument back, Lock back), then hold. Test: press a drop during a drop-out and check the instrument is on from the build's first bar.

**S2. The no-repeat check passes on parameter drift alone; the notes repeat.** `src/engine/index.ts:1621-1633` (the bar signature includes every macro position and patch value Morph moves), `src/engine/p11.test.ts:50`, `tools/morphcheck.py:177-178`. Probe with the notes alone (same 160 bars): Ambient repeats a 16-bar stretch of notes 12 times (36 distinct bars), Drone 11 times (17 distinct bars). Bright moves 0.01 of the dial a bar, so two stretches can differ only by an inaudible cutoff step. "Exactly" allows the builder's reading, but it is an open choice that decides whether the Done-when line means anything to the ear. Fix: either record it under Choices in the changelog for Pedro's veto ("a 16-bar stretch counts as repeated only when its notes and every Morph value are equal; the notes alone repeat about 12 times in ten minutes of Ambient"), or tighten the check to the notes and the instruments on, and make voicing or drop-out moves frequent enough to pass it. At minimum the first.

### Minor

**M1. The core part's Pad -> Arp stall ("read -177 ms after the switch") is a race in the check, not P11's logic.** `tools/smoke.py:1501-1551` (`walk_pad_to_arp`): the in-page loop sees the scheduler place the filter move, then sleeps in 1 ms steps until 15 ms before it; the switch it just made (`setLayerMode`) triggers a Preact re-render and the 0.4 s autosave on the same main thread, and a headless container under load can block it longer than the scheduler's lookahead (about one sixteenth, 123 ms at 122 bpm), so the read lands after the move. P11 adds little to that path (with no vibe Morph is off and only counts the bar; the pad page now renders six dials, not five). The check fails loudly ("could not run") rather than passing, so it cannot hide a fault. Fix: when `ahead <= 0`, rerun the measurement (up to two more times) on the next bar line before failing, as it is a timing miss and not a reading of the filter; print each attempt.

**M2. No test that Morph moves again after a drop ends.** `src/engine/p11.test.ts:170` checks no move from the press but the dry engine never runs a drop (no audio graph, the drop stays pressed), and `tools/morphcheck.py:247-262` checks no move in the held bars only. Fix: in the smoke's drop step, assert Morph makes a move within a few bars after the wind-down's end (`morphMoves` with `bar > held[-1]`).

**M3. "Morph off plays the notes the sequencer plays without it" compares two P11 paths.** `src/engine/p11.test.ts:139`: both runs go through `voiceNotes` (voicing missing against 0.5), so it cannot catch a change from before P11, e.g. if a chord's notes were ever not ascending, `voiceNotes`' sort would reorder a drone's or swoop pad's glide pairs. Fix: add an assertion that `voiceNotes(notes, 0)` returns `notes` unchanged for every chord of every vibe's sections (the ascending invariant of `chords.ts:8`).

**M4. Spec 8.9 and 8.12 still say DROP is full width.** `docs/SPEC.md:343` ("full width") and section 8.12's "DROP is a full-width yellow tile", against 8.6's "with the Morph switch beside it". Not a P11 code fault. Fix: the closing chat adds "(beside the Morph switch, spec 8.6)" to those two lines as a note, or records it in the changelog.

**M5. Backlog row R1 says "the full six-part smoke".** `docs/BACKLOG.md` row R1 (main chat's text, not the builder's): there are now seven parts. Fix: "seven-part" when R1 is worked.

## Spec rules (section 7, phone first)

- Audio behind `src/engine`: yes. No UI file imports Tone.js; the new UI (`src/ui/morph.tsx`, tab edits) calls the engine only.
- Seeded randomness: yes. The planner draws only from `createRng(deriveSeed(seed, n))`, one stream per thing, so a held control does not change the others' journeys. `textSeed` is a hash, not a random choice. No `Math.random`, no timers.
- Parameter changes ramped: macro moves go through `setMacro`/`setParams` (ramped as a hand's); a voicing change reaches a held drone or swooping pad as a glide (`reshape`), a plain pad on its next chord. No stepped change found.
- Stuck notes: none found; toggles use the queued bar-line path, voicing keeps the voice count.
- Changes queued to the bar line: Morph's toggles and section moves are made at the bar line before the sequencer plays it.
- 48 px, no hover: Morph switch 104 x 48 (short screens) or 56; Morph page switches 48; no `:hover` added. Simple words only on Simple screens ("Morph", "Morph speed").
- Drop overlay and P10's song-document guarantee: Morph makes no move from the press to the end of the wind-down (unit test and smoke), so the drop leaves the document as P10 promised; S1 is the one gap.
- Morph beside DROP (8.6, 8.12): yes, on DROP's row, its icon `all_inclusive`, turning a quarter a bar while on and playing, not turning under the Still motion setting.
