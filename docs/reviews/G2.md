## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Mix tab half off screen at 360 px.** `src/style.css:717`. `grid-template-columns: repeat(8, minmax(var(--hit), 1fr))` needs 384 px; at 360 px the last tab is clipped and only reachable by a horizontal swipe on a bar with no visible scrollbar. The Done-when "a whole jam with one thumb" (sweep a filter from Mix) fails on a 360 px phone. Fix: below 384 px lay the bar out as two rows of four (`@media (max-width: 383px) { .tabs { grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr); } .tab { min-height: 52px; } }`), keeping every tab at least 48 x 48 and all eight visible; record the choice in the changelog. Add a 360 px pass to the smoke test (see S4) that fails if any `.tab` extends past the viewport.

### Should-fix

**S1. Out-of-key bass after a key or mood change.** `src/engine/index.ts:296-297`. On `retrigger` only synth, layer1 and layer2 are released. With the `long` bass rhythm (`src/music/presets.ts:19-21`, notes of 7.5 steps) the old root keeps sounding for up to half a bar in the new key (spec section 5: no out-of-key notes). Fix: on `r.retrigger` also call `g.instruments.bass.releaseAll(time)`; in `Sequencer.next` (`src/engine/sequencer.ts` bass block) emit a bass note at the new root on a retrigger step when the rhythm is mid-note, with `len` the rest of that hit. Add a test: key change mid long-note yields a bass event in the new scale and `retrigger` true.

**S2. Pending outline drops before the bar line at fast tempos.** `src/ui/App.tsx:81` and `:88`. The UI reads `engine.pending()` live, but the sequencer flushes the queue when it schedules step 0, about 0.1 s (Tone's lookahead) before the bar line is heard. At 174 bpm a sixteenth is 86 ms, so the step-15 draw already sees an empty queue while the song (re-read only at drawn step 0) still shows the old state: for one step the cell or column header shows neither pending nor the new state. Fix: have the engine attach the pending snapshot to the scheduled `Position` (taken before `seq.next()` flushes) and have the UI use that, or keep the last flushed `Pending` visible until the drawn position reaches its bar. Test it in the sequencer/engine at 174 bpm.

**S3. Buffer choice reloads the page and loses the jam.** `src/ui/tabs/MixTab.tsx:30`. Each buffer button is a link to `?hint=...`; tapping one while playing reloads and throws away every unsaved edit (there is no saving until G5). Fix: disable the three buttons while playing, or ask for a second tap ("Tap again to reload; edits are lost"), and say in the label that the song resets.

**S4. Smoke tests only at 390 px.** `tools/smoke.py:191`. The size check never sees a 360 px screen, which is how E1 passed. Fix: after the walk, set the viewport to 360 x 740, open every tab again, run `check_sizes`, and fail if any button's right edge exceeds the viewport width.

**S5. Section strip logic not unit-tested.** `src/ui/App.tsx:104`, `:162`, `:164`. The rule that a chosen section reverts to following when it becomes the playing one or disappears, and the strip's `isEdit` and label rules, are inline in the component. Fix: move them to `src/ui/logic.ts` (for example `stripState(i, chosen, playing, isPlaying)` and `keepChosen(chosen, playing, count)`) and add tests in `src/ui/logic.test.ts`.

### Minor

**M1. Transport can overflow its padding.** `src/style.css:114-147`. With `flex: none` on every pill, a 6-character key label ("C# phr") and a 5-character bar ("100.4") need about 339 px of the 332 available at 360 px, pushing Record into the right padding. Fix: give `.pill.bar` `flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: var(--hit)` and the key pill `min-width: 0` with ellipsis, or shorten the bar label to the bar number after bar 99.

**M2. Pending cell does not show which way it is going.** `src/style.css:346-350`. On-to-off and off-to-on pending cells look the same. Fix: for `.cell.on.pending` keep the outline and add a small filled corner or a diagonal stripe in `var(--c)`, so "about to turn off" differs from "about to turn on"; still outlined, not filled.

**M3. Two full re-renders per XY move.** `src/ui/tabs/InstrumentTab.tsx:220-223` with `src/engine/index.ts:128`. Each pointer move calls `setParam` twice, and each call fires `changed()`, which clones the whole song and re-renders the shell. Fix: add `engine.setParams(inst, { cutoff, resonance })` that fires `changed()` once, or coalesce change notifications to one per animation frame.

**M4. Engine re-index path untested.** `src/engine/index.ts` `changed(true)` and the `layoutGen` check in the scheduled draw. Sequencer remap is tested; the engine's correction of a stale scheduled position is not. Fix: cover it in the smoke walk (copy section A while B plays, then check `position().section` still names Groove).

**M5. 9 px text.** `src/style.css:270` (`.corner`) and `:731` (`.tab`). Hard to read at arm's length on a phone. Fix: 11 px at least for both.

## Verdict

**fix.** 1 error, 5 should-fix, 5 minor. The shell matches the spec's Play grid and section-strip behaviour and keeps section 7; the one Done-when failure is the clipped Mix tab at 360 px.
