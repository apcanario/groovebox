## Findings

### Errors

**E1. `tools/shots.py` fails on HEAD: the tablet walk's "pads kept playing" check assumes every section starts a note within two bars.** `tools/shots.py` lines 789 to 791. In `shots.py` the tablet walk runs after the live walk, so Ambient is loaded; Ambient's sections A and D (Synth Held plus Layer 2 Drone only) start no note in 16 s (probed: `attacks()` unchanged over 16 s in both, every bar for B and C). The walk advances the section twice per size, so 1280 x 800 and 800 x 1280 land on A or D and fail ("the pads stopped or left the screen while the panel was used", 4 lines); the smoke's core part plays a different vibe and passes. The app is not at fault; the check measures the wrong thing. Fix: judge "the pads kept playing" by what the line claims, not by attacks: the position advanced (bar or step moved) and `running` stayed true across the pad tap and the dial drag, plus the level staying above the floor `walk_rotate` uses; or load a vibe whose every section starts notes each bar (the default Groove section) before `walk_tablet` in `main()` (line 845). Do not lengthen the wait.

**E2. A 600 x 960 tablet (the left edge of spec 9.1's table, a common 7-inch tablet) cuts off the panel.** `src/style.css` lines 2464 to 2480 (portrait dock sizing) with `src/ui/size.ts` line 21. At 600 x 960 in Simple, Layer 1's XY pad is cut off at the dock's top edge (body overflows 53 px; Drums 35 px; Advanced Layer 1 23 px), and the header's vibe name shrinks to about 9 px ("Pick a vibe"). Spec 9.1: "Nothing scrolls on a tablet"; the row: "nothing is cut off, text fits its tile". The four row sizes pass, but the layout table makes 600 px a tablet. Fix: size the dock to the height left (rows of `--dockrow` down to 48 px, the punch space kept at 48 px rather than 56 px under about 1000 px of height) and let the panel take its compact phone form under a measured height (the `roomy` mechanism, which already exists); give the header's vibe control at least the 10 px text floor of spec 8.6, or drop "All 24 vibes" under 700 px wide. Add 600 x 960 to the smoke's tablet sizes (`ROTATE_SIZES` already has a list; `TABLET_SIZES` in `tools/shots.py` line 590) so it stays fixed.

### Should-fix

**S1. Square tablets and wide tablets under 900 px get the portrait dock, which does not fit them.** `src/ui/size.ts` lines 21 to 26 (the choice "any other tablet ... is portrait"). 900 x 900: Layer 1 body overflows 113 px, its New idea row half cut off. 880 x 620: the dock takes the whole height and the panel is gone (body overflows 166 to 345 px on every panel). Spec 9.1's table has no row for these, so this is the builder's choice; as built it breaks "nothing cut off" on them. Fix: pick by fit, not only by width: wider than tall and at least 600 px tall goes to the desk (landscape) from about 800 px wide (the desk at 1024 x 768 has room to spare: 58/42 split, 124 px row heads, 4 sections), and a square screen goes to the desk when its height cannot hold header + 300 px panel + dock. Add one of these sizes to the walk. Pedro may also veto the choice outright (see Choices).

**S2. The "without changing screen" line is not asserted.** `tools/shots.py` lines 758 to 792. The walk taps a pad and drags a dial, then checks the pads are still in the DOM, but never checks that the panel stayed open and the layout stayed: a pad tap that opened another panel (as a row label tap does) would pass. Fix: before the tap read `.inst` `data-panel` and `document.documentElement.dataset.size`; after the pad tap and again after the drag, fail if either changed or `.inst .body .dial` is gone.

### Minor

**M1. The landscape instrument half has no bottom margin.** `src/style.css` line 2283 (`.tablet.landscape .inst`). Its last control (the Simple XY pad, Advanced Layer's Delay and Reverb sends) sits on the screen's bottom edge at 1280 x 800 and 1024 x 768, while the pads half keeps 8 px under the deck. Fix: `padding-bottom: 8px` (plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) on `.tablet.landscape .inst`.

**M2. "Next bar" under a dock lane label overlaps the hatched frame at 48 px rows.** Seen at 768 x 1024 Simple (`--dockrow: clamp(48px, 4.7vh, 64px)`, `src/style.css` line 2471, gives 48 px): the word sits on the frame's bottom hatching and reads clipped. At 800 x 1280 (60 px rows) it is clear. Fix: raise the floor of `--dockrow` to 52 px where the panel has room (it does at 768 x 1024: Mix and Chords leave 50 to 150 px empty), or pad the pending NowTile's text inside the frame.

**M3. Two unit tests are named for more than they check.** `src/ui/size.test.ts` line 94 ("a rotation never touches the engine") only checks that `size.ts` imports nothing; `src/ui/desk.test.ts` line 61 matches a regex in `App.tsx` source. The real guarantee is the smoke's `walk_rotate` (engine `onChange` count, song, landed switches, level, attacks), which is sound. Fix: rename them to what they check ("the size layer imports nothing"), or drop the regex test.

**M4. Simple panels on a tablet are the phone's panels.** Spec 9.2: "Simple panels are the picker, the four macro dials at a large size, and the XY pad." The macro dials stay at phone size (about 84 to 104 px) in both layouts and Simple Mix keeps its four pages with half the panel empty at 800 x 1280. Not a T2 item (T2 is Advanced). Fix: on a tablet size the Simple macro dials up (for example `grid-auto-rows: 120px` in the desk's instrument half) and show Simple Mix's Master and Levels together where the body has the height; or carry it to T2 with a line.

**M5. `ROOMY_PORTRAIT_PX` (640) has no test.** `src/ui/App.tsx` line 65. The rule (at 800 x 1280 the panel has about 575 px, so the XY pad and sends page) is only covered by the screenshots. Fix: a unit test on a pure `roomyFor(layout, height)` helper, or note it in the smoke's DOCK check.
