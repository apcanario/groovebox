## Errors

**E1. Crush and Shifter worklet processors never stop, so they pile up and the load grows with use.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 109 and 156 (`return true` always), 185-190, 277-287 (`dispose` only disconnects and closes the port), 908-909 (every switch-off frees the effect and pre-builds a fresh one), 919-921 (every effect pick builds one).
- A processor that returns `true` is kept alive and keeps running after it is disconnected.
- The pre-built copy of an off slot also runs. Every song has a Crush in Drums slot 1 by default, so an off slot does not "cost nothing" (spec 8.8).
- Measured with the transport stopped: load 31.8%. After 300 Crush/Shifter builds: 82.5%. After 1200: 99.9%. As a control, 600 Chorus/Phaser builds left the load at 32.9%, and 100 more Crush builds then raised it to 53.4%.
- A jam that toggles Crush or browses the picker, or a run of vibe changes, saturates the phone's audio thread.
- **Fix:**
  - Give both processors a `done` flag set by a port message in `dispose`, and `return false` once it is set.
  - Do not create the worklet node until the effect is wired. Build it in `Crush`/`Shifter.start`, or in `FxSlotNode.wire`, so a pre-built, unwired copy holds no processor.
  - Add a smoke check: with the transport stopped, load before and after 100 Crush/Shifter picks and on/off switches, within 3 points.

**E2. "Switched mid-jam without a click (tested by level continuity)": the test would not fail for several effects or for any instrument but Layer 2.** `tools/smoke.py` 2357-2425.
- The reference is `max(dry, wet)`, where wet is the effect's own steady sound. For Crush (staircase) and Fuzz (clipped edges) it is 7.8x and 6.8x the dry drone's, which raises the click threshold by the same factor.
- The proof that the check can fail was run on Vowel only (at full mix). My probe repeated it with each effect's default knobs, 8 stepped and 8 ramped switches each:

  | Effect | Stepped switches caught |
  |---|---|
  | Crush | 1 of 8 |
  | Gate | 1 of 8 |
  | Ring | 2 of 8 |
  | Fuzz | 4 of 8 |
  | Vowel | 5 of 8 |
  | Echo | 6 of 8 |
  | Chorus | 7 of 8 |
  | Tremolo | 7 of 8 |
  | Shifter | 8 of 8 |

  Ramped switches caught nothing for any effect.
- For Crush, Gate and Ring, a stepped (clicking) implementation would pass. The "7 of 8 caught" figure is the best case, not the sensitivity.
- **Fix:**
  - Run the stepped-switch proof for every effect, and fail when any effect's check catches fewer than 5 of 8.
  - For effects whose wet sound has its own large jumps, set knobs for the check that keep the wet smooth and the step large (Crush 12 bits at the top rate with Mix 1; Fuzz with Tone low; Ring with Mix 1; Gate with Depth 1 on an open step). Or take the reference from the dry drone alone, with the wet compared through a high-passed jump.
  - Run the switch check on Bass as well (long notes on a sine patch), not only on Layer 2.

**E3. The lo-fi master's on/off (now a P9 Simple control) has no working click test, and the P7 drone-switch check the row leans on is weaker than it reads.** `tools/smoke.py` 2171-2244, `src/engine/continuity.ts` 11, 20-28.
- **Master checks are blind.** Lo-fi, air and the 20 s reverb are judged on the master while the whole groove plays. The master's steady jump-over-peak measured 0.29 to 0.33, 11 times the drone's 0.027. A step must exceed about 0.75 of the master peak to count, so a real click passes. The P9 walk only clicks `#lofi` (2481-2486) and checks no continuity.
- **The first read after a watch starts can inflate the reference.** The watch's `last` starts at 0, so the first sample after it is created registers a jump equal to that sample. The Layer 2 watch is first created inside `steady = steady_jumps(page, "layer2", 2.0)`. In my probe that first reference read 0.19 against a settled 0.027 (7 times), which would make every P7 Layer 2 check that run near-blind. This is racy: it depends on whether the first quantum lands in a kept window.
- **What "largest jump 3.03 times the steady sound's" means.** For the wave changes (2237-2244), the check compares against the new wave's own reference, `max(prev, new)`. The printed `worst` multiplies back to the sine drone's reference, so a sawtooth's own edge shows up as a "jump". The sawtooth's steady is 0.064, 2.4 times the sine's 0.027. 3.03 is a sawtooth or noise wave's ordinary sound plus noise, not a click, and that is why it passed. The printed "largest jump" mixes references and is not the measure that was judged.
- **What is sound.** The P7 Layer 2 switches (drift, LFO, swoop, fifth, rate changes) against a correctly taken sine reference: there a step of about 7% of peak is caught.
- **Fix:**
  - In `GrooveJump`, take `last` from the first sample seen (no jump on the first sample).
  - Take `steady` as the median of the windows, not the maximum.
  - Run the lo-fi, air and reverb switches with every instrument but the drone switched off, so the master reference is the drone's. Add the same check to the P9 Lo-fi button click.
  - Print each check's ratio against its own reference.

## Should-fix

**S1. The "six on" cost is one light set of six.** `tools/smoke.py` 2045-2051, `docs/SPEC.md` 8.8.
- Under the cap, `fx` keeps the last six: synth Chorus and Shifter, Layer 1 Flanger and Vowel, Layer 2 Ring and Tremolo.
- Drums Crush, Drums Echo, Bass Fuzz and Bass Phaser are never part of a measured six.
- 68 to 69% sits at the top of P7's 54 to 71%, so a heavier six may pass 71%.
- **Fix:** add a scene `fx6max` with the heaviest six (Crush, Shifter, Echo, Flanger, Vowel, Phaser, chosen by a one-effect-at-a-time measure). Report it in spec 8.8 with the set named.

**S2. Tempo-synced effects follow the tempo in code but nothing tests it.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 385-388, 530-532, 709-711, 780-782; `src/engine/audio.ts` 292-296.
- **Fix:** expose the current LFO rate and Echo time per slot in `fxState()`. Add a unit-level or smoke check: Tremolo at 1/4 and Echo at 1/8; `setBpm` from 120 to 90; the rate goes from 2.0 to 1.5 Hz and the Echo time from 0.25 to 0.333 s. Do the same for a vibe load at another tempo.

**S3. Changing a slot's effect during its switch-off fade cuts the old effect at once.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 921.
- `if (!this.on) return this.free(old)` runs while `old.wet` is still fading (the 90 ms window), which is a step.
- A switch-off followed quickly by a pick, a replayed log, or a vibe load can hit it.
- **Fix:** when `old.wired`, keep it until its fade ends (`afterFade(() => this.free(old), RAMP * 3, at)`, counted in `leaving`), and build the new effect unwired.

**S4. The knob check moves only the first knob, and only on Layer 2.** `tools/smoke.py` 2376-2383.
- Never checked while playing: Echo's Time (a delay-time glide), the rate knobs (Phaser and Flanger crossing from Hz to a note value), Vowel's Rate from Still to moving, Gate's Pattern, Shifter's Shift and Ring's Pitch with Follow.
- **Fix:** turn each of the three knobs of every effect, low to high, with the continuity check (after E2's reference fix).

## Minor

**M1. LFO phase jump on Play.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 382-384, 527-529, 706-708, with `Lfo.start` stopping and replacing the oscillator at `at`.
- On transport start, a Tremolo, Pan, Vowel or synced sweep that is already on steps its phase. If the instrument still rings (quick Stop then Play), the gain or pan steps.
- **Fix:** restart only when the instrument is silent, or crossfade (fade the slot's wet a few ms around `at`).

**M2. Gate's Smooth goes down to 1 ms.** `src/music/effects.ts` 115.
- A 1 ms time constant on a sustained pad clicks on every open and close. The square Shape of Tremolo and Pan at fast rates does the same.
- **Fix:** floor `gateTau` at about 3 ms, and keep the square shape's tanh soft enough that a transition is no shorter than 3 ms.

**M3. Slot 2 is never checked for audibility or disconnection.** `tools/smoke.py` 2328-2350 use slot 1 (index 0) only.
- **Fix:** run the same loop on slot 2 for one instrument.

**M4. Activation order is lost on load.** `src/engine/index.ts` 533 (`takeFx`) rebuilds the order in instrument order, so after a reload the "oldest" slot is Drums', not the one switched on first.
- **Fix:** store the order in the song (an optional `fxOrder`), or say in spec 8.8 that a loaded song counts in instrument order.

**M5. Ring's Follow on an empty synth event.** `src/engine/index.ts` 1179: `Math.min(...e.notes)` on an empty array gives `Infinity`. With Follow above 0, `setTargetAtTime(Infinity)` throws inside `tick`.
- **Fix:** guard it with `e.notes.length` as the layer branch does.

**M6. Shifter's output is mono.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 141, 723 (`outputChannelCount: [1]`, input channel 0 only). Placed after a Pan, Chorus or Echo in slot 1, it drops the right channel and collapses the width.
- **Fix:** process each input channel with its own Hilbert state.

**M7. Echo switched off cuts its tail.** `src/engine/fx.ts` 900-913. The wet fades in about 30 ms and the effect is freed after 90 ms. There is no click, but the repeats stop dead.
- **Fix:** for Echo, close only its input on switch-off, and free it once the feedback has decayed (about 1.5 s). Pedro's ears decide.

**M8. Mix Space page and shots coverage.** `src/ui/tabs/MixTab.tsx` 138-155.
- The group label "Lo-fi" sits over a button that also says "Lo-fi", with an empty band above it at 360 x 640.
- `tools/shots.py` has no screenshot of the effect picker sheet.
- **Fix:** drop the group label, or name the button "On"/"Off". Add a picker-sheet shot.

**M9. The watches read the left channel only.** `src/engine/continuity.ts` 21, 45. A click from Pan or Echo on the right channel is unseen.
- **Fix:** take the larger of both channels in `GrooveJump` and `GrooveDiff`.

**M10. LFO rate changes step.** `src/engine/mods.ts` 111: `Lfo.rate` uses `setValueAtTime` (section 7: parameter changes are ramped). It is inaudible as a click, since the phase is continuous. After a rate or tempo change, though, a synced LFO stays off the bar by a fixed fraction.
- **Fix:** ramp the frequency over one render quantum or more, and re-phase on the next transport start.

## Verdict

**fix.** 3 errors, 4 should-fix, 10 minor.

The effect graph, crossfades, cap, vibes and UI are sound. E1 is a real CPU leak with a local fix. E2 and E3 are test gaps that leave the "(tested)" Done-when line unproven for Crush, Gate and Ring, and the lo-fi switch untested.
