## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Bass Punch moves a value Advanced never shows.** `src/music/macros.ts:109` (bass Punch target `attack`), `src/ui/logic.ts` `KNOB_PAGES.bass` (no attack dial).
Bass Punch writes `patches.bass.attack` (0.03 to 0.001 s), which the bass voice plays (`audio.ts` `setEnvelope`), but no Advanced bass page has an attack dial. Spec 8.1: "Advanced shows the result of every Simple move"; the row's Done-when says the same.
Fix: add `{ key: "attack", label: "Attack", seconds: [0.001, 0.1] }` to `KNOB_PAGES.bass` (the page has five dials, room for six), and extend the read-back in `walk_p2` (`tools/smoke.py:1026`) to every macro target, not three: after each Simple macro turn, switch to Advanced and check each target's dial shows the song's value. Add a unit test that every patch key a macro targets appears in `KNOB_PAGES`, `PAD_KNOBS`, the generative knobs, the arp controls, the filter panel or the Mix levels.

**E2. Simple Chords shows chord degrees.** `src/ui/tabs/ChordsTab.tsx:122` and `:138` (`c.degree + 1`).
In Simple the palette and the sequence read 1 to 7: these are scale degrees by another name. Spec 8.1 bans chord degrees in Simple, and the row asks for every visible Simple string to be checked. Visible in `shots/360x640-simple-chords-chords.png`.
Fix: in Simple show no number: draw each chord as a coloured block (colour by degree, as 8.4's cards will) with its length under it, and keep the numbers for Advanced. P4 replaces this tab, but until then Simple must not show degrees.

### Should-fix

**S1. C4 is cleared only for new songs; old saved jams get worse.** `src/engine/song.ts:277`, `src/music/sound.ts` (`PAD_TRIM`), `src/engine/sequencer.ts:733`.
`PAD_TRIM` (-12 dB) applies to every song, but Layer 2's level and envelope change only in `defaultPatch`. A saved jam (including the autosaved one that opens on launch) keeps Layer 2 at level 0.22 with the 0.7 s attack, so its Layer 2 pad now sits about 12 dB further under Layer 1 than before P2, and its lead and arp stay 10 to 16 dB under. Smoke shows the same once patches are moved off the defaults: the G4 walk reads layer1 generative -19.8 dB against layer2 generative -36.0 and layer2 pad -42.1. The C4 walk measures only the default patches.
Fix: on load, when Layer 2's patch still equals the old default (`level 0.22, attack 0.7, decay 0.6, sustain 0.8, release 1.8`), replace those five values with the new defaults; test it with the base commit's default song as a fixture. If that is not wanted, add a Carried line saying old jams keep the old balance.

**S2. Two "Bright" controls on one Simple screen write the same value.** `src/ui/tabs/SimpleTab.tsx:49` (XY `xLabel="Bright"`), `src/music/macros.ts:42`, `:92`, `:120`.
On Bass, Synth and both Layer tabs the Bright dial and the XY pad's "Bright" axis both write `cutoff`. Moving the pad leaves the Bright dial at its stored position, so the screen shows two different answers for one value, both labelled Bright. Spec 8.3 lets Advanced edits leave the dial where it was, not another Simple control beside it.
Fix: give the XY's X axis a different plain word (for example "Tone"), and when the Simple XY moves `cutoff`, clear the stored position of any macro whose first target is `cutoff` so the dial reads the value again (`macroPosition` falls back to it). Add a unit test.

**S3. New idea and Back do nothing heard in Arp mode.** `src/ui/tabs/SimpleTab.tsx:112`, `:119`; `src/engine/sequencer.ts` `newSeed`/`prevSeed` and `enterSection` (`arpRng`).
The buttons are enabled in Arp, but the arp's generator is reseeded from `gen.seed` only when a section is entered, and only the random direction uses it. Tapping New idea in Arp changes the seed and the history with no audible change. Smoke tests the buttons in Lead mode only.
Fix: either reseed `arpRng` for that layer in `newSeed` and `prevSeed` when the layer is in Arp and the section is playing, or disable both buttons in Arp as in Pad. Add the Arp case to the unit tests.

**S4. The plain-words check covers only the instrument tabs.** `tools/smoke.py:995` and `:997`.
`jargon` is checked on the five instrument tabs only, so Chords (E2), Mix, Play and both sheets in Simple are never checked; that is how E2 passed.
Fix: run the check on every Simple screen the frame walk visits (all tabs, pages and sheets), and add a rule that a Simple chord tile shows no digit 1 to 7 alone.

### Minor

**M1. The Back test covers one alignment and the sequencer only.** `src/engine/p2.test.ts:156`.
It calls Back on `Sequencer` at a bar where the chord loop restarts. Add a case through `Engine.prevSeed` (the path the UI uses), and one with Back pressed mid-bar, checking the lead's notes from the next bar line.

**M2. The min/max macro test passes for a macro with no targets.** `src/engine/p2.test.ts` "each macro at minimum and at maximum...".
Add `expect(m.targets.length).toBeGreaterThan(0)` inside the loop.

**M3. Busy is inert in Pad and Evolve is inert in Arp.** `src/music/macros.ts:44`, `:54`.
Busy maps lead and arp values only; Evolve maps Lock and pad motion only. On those modes the dial moves and nothing is heard. Record it under Choices in the changelog, or give each a target that mode plays (for example Evolve on an arp moving its gate or octaves).

**M4. Bass Punch has no drive.** `src/music/macros.ts:105`.
Spec 8.3 says Punch moves attack, decay and drive; the builder gave drive to Weight only, to avoid two dials on one value. Reasonable, but it differs from the spec: record it under Choices in the changelog (RUN.md 1).

**M5. The XY dot is cut off at the pad's edge.** `shots/360x640-simple-drums.png` (100 · 0, a quarter of the dot shows), `shots/360x640-simple-layer2-*.png` (Edge 10, half the dot).
Inset the dot's travel by its radius, or let it draw over the pad's edge, so it is whole at every value.

**M6. Simple words to check.** `src/ui/logic.ts` `PRESET_LABEL` (`DnB`, `DnB sub`), `src/ui/tabs/MixTab.tsx:95` (Simple dial named "Filter").
"DnB" and "sub" are abbreviations a non-musician may not read; "Filter" on the Mix Master page in Simple is a synth term. Suggest "Drum and bass", "Deep", "Tone" in Simple.

**M7. Simple Layer tab against the look.** `src/style.css` `.insttab.simple > .lane` (40 px, 52 px when roomy).
Against `docs/design/look/groovebox-v3-phone-2-simple-layer.jpg` the lane is about half the reference's height and the XY pad takes the spare room at 412 x 915. The lead-style row and the two effect buttons are missing, but they belong to P5 and P9. Give the spare height to the lane first.

**M8. Stray marker in the layer mode switch.** `shots/412x915-simple-layer1-pad.png`: a thin purple bar sits between Lead and Arp when Pad is chosen (the sliding marker left behind, or a border). Check `.modegroup` styles in Simple.
