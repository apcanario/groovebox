## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Drift has no long rests.** `src/music/leadDrift.ts:191` (`rest = 2 * round(((2 + r*10) * (1 - 0.8*busy)) / 2)`). Spec 8.5: "Sparse long notes ... with long rests." Probe over 16 bars at seed 1234 (gaps between one note's end and the next onset, in steps): Busy 0 gives 2, 4, -5 (an overlap); Busy 0.5 gives 2, 2, 8; Busy 0.85 (the default song) gives 0, 2, 2, 0; Busy 1 gives all 0. The printed Drift is a near-continuous line, and no Busy setting gives a rest longer than half a bar. No test checks rests. Fix: make rests long and let Busy only shorten them, for example `rest = 4 * round((4 + r * 8) * (1 - 0.6 * busy))` (a beat at Busy 1, about one to three bars at Busy 0, at least half a bar at the default). Add a test: at Busy at or under 0.85, every phrase of 4 bars holds at least one rest of 8 or more steps, and no gap is negative.

### Should-fix

**S1. Drift does not move by step.** `src/music/leadDrift.ts:162,197` with `realize` in `src/music/leadKit.ts:204-212`. STEP is 0.14 of the window's height, not one scale degree, and `realize` then snaps to the nearest chord tone (65% of notes at Tension 0.35). Probe: leaps of 2 to 5 semitones at default range (2,4,4,2,...), and up to 5 at Range 1. Thirds and fourths, not steps. The test at `src/music/leadStyles.test.ts:204` passes leaps of up to 7 semitones, so it would not catch this. Fix: move `h` by one scale degree of the window (`1 / (scaleNotesInWindow - 1)`, passed in or derived from Range). For Drift, snap to the nearest pool note only when that note is within two semitones of the step target; otherwise use the scale note, which is still in key. Tighten the test to at least 75% of moves of 2 semitones or less (0 allowed).

**S2. A glide can bend the wrong note when the layer runs out of voices.** `src/engine/audio.ts:715-722`. The code takes the last entry of Tone's private `_activeVoices` to be the voice just started. When PolySynth is at `maxPolyphony` (10 on the layers) it drops the note with a warning and starts no voice. The last entry is then another note still sounding, and its pitch is cancelled and slid from `glideFrom` to the new note: an audible wrong bend on a held note. This is reachable: Roll at Repeats 1 starts up to three voices per sixteenth, Note length 1 holds 2.7 steps, and layer release goes up to 8 s (`song.ts:91`). Fix: read `_activeVoices.length` before `triggerAttackRelease`. Glide only if the length grew and the new last entry's `midi` equals `notes[0]`. Count the drop for the smoke test.

**S3. The accent's 10 kHz cap is computed from the cutoff target, not the cutoff sounding.** `src/engine/audio.ts:192-193`. `base` is `cutoffHz(cutoffValue)`, the XY pad's target. While the cutoff ramps down (a drag from bright to dark), the native frequency is still above the target, and up to 1200 cents more takes the filter past 10 kHz: from 10 kHz toward 1 kHz, about 20 kHz for some 0.1 s. This breaks C1's cap. Fix: `const base = Math.max(this.cutoffHz(this.cutoffValue), nativeValue(this.filter.frequency))`. Optionally scale the boost down while a cutoff ramp is under way.

### Minor

**M1. A Drift phrase's last note spills into the next phrase.** `src/music/leadDrift.ts:189-192`. The loop starts notes up to `steps - 4` with lengths up to 48, so the last note overlaps the next phrase's first note (gap -5 at Busy 0). With Phrase 1 bar, three notes overlap. Fix: clamp `len` to `steps - at` for the last note, unless it slides.

**M2. A repeat cuts a slide short.** `src/engine/index.ts:924-931`. The glide (0.8 of a sixteenth, at most 90 ms) runs on the first hit, but the second and third hits start a sub-sixteenth later on new voices already at the target pitch, so the slide is heard for 40 to 60 ms and then jumps. Fix: when a note has both `glideFrom` and `repeat`, glide over `0.8 * sub`, or skip the repeat on slid notes (`decorate`, `src/music/leadKit.ts:167`).

**M3. An accent brightens every sounding note of the layer.** `src/engine/audio.ts:186-201`. The boost is on the channel filter's detune, so a note held under it is brightened too: Hook and Drift long notes, a slid note, Pulse slides. Spec 8.5 asks for a per-note boost. Accept and note it in the changelog as a lead-is-monophonic approximation, or say so in the `accent` comment.

**M4. setGen cannot clear a P5 field.** `src/engine/sequencer.ts:403-407`. `style: undefined` (and `shape` or `phrase` undefined) is refused, so a caller cannot return a layer to "unset, style default". The smoke walk works around this by writing explicit `pulse`/`0` values into the song. Fix: treat `undefined` as delete-the-key for the optional fields, and test it.

**M5. The Phrase style at Phrase 1 bar is degenerate.** `src/music/leadPhrase.ts:60-64`. Each part is 4 steps, 2 of them active, so the "line" is one or two notes per beat with two-step rests. Spec 8.5 describes Phrase as "a line over 4 or 8 bars". Fix: for the Phrase style, treat Phrase 1 and 2 as 2 bars minimum (parts of half a bar), or document the floor in the changelog.
