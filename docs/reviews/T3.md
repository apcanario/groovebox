# Review: T3, punch pads, movement and many fingers

Reviewer: fresh Opus 5.5 agent. Diff `769ce27..4ae8769` (slices a, b, c), against `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/RUN.md` sections 3 and 4c, spec 9 (all) and 8.12, row T3, Carried C31 and C32, `docs/reviews/T1.md`, `T2.md` and `T3-measure.md`.

## Verdict

**fix.** 2 errors, 3 should-fix, 9 minor. The timing is right for a single press, and so are the pads, the many-finger walk and the off switch. A quick second press breaks it: a second tap of Echo or Wash before its start beat silences it (E1), and a hold pad pressed again before its start line comes in off the grid (E2). Neither case is tested.

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. A second tap of Echo or Wash before its start beat makes it silent for the whole bar, while the pad shows it lit.** `src/engine/punch.ts` line 177 (`live` is true as soon as an end is scheduled, even when the start is still ahead) with line 340 (`hold(send.gain, this.ctx.currentTime)`). The first tap schedules the send's opening ramp on the next beat. A second tap before that beat counts as "live", so it holds the send at its current value, which is 0. That cancels the opening ramp, and then it schedules the close. Probe, run on the fake clock at 120 bpm: a tap at 10.10 s, then another at 10.25 s, gives `punchState` `{start: 10.5, end: 12.5}`, but the echo send reads 0 at 10.6 s and at 11.5 s. The wash send reads 0 at 11 s. Failing scenario: a double tap on Echo, which is the natural way to "throw" a delay, gives no echo. Spec 9.4: "One tap throws the mix into the delay for a bar." The test at `punch.test.ts` line 158 re-taps only after the start (11.2 s), so it passes. Fix: count a tap as live only once its start has passed (`s.start <= now`). Before that, a second tap keeps the start that is already scheduled and only moves the end, by `landAt(send.gain, 0, end)` after a `hold` at the old end minus R, never at now. Add a test: two taps inside the first beat, with the send at 1 on the start line.

**E2. A hold pad let go and pressed again before its start line comes in at once, off the grid. Stutter then loops with no loop length set.** `src/engine/punch.ts` lines 177 and 189 to 193 with `resume` (lines 264 to 295), which lands everything at `now + R`. Probe at 120 bpm: press at 10.30 s, release at 10.31 s, press at 10.32 s.
- Cut: drums and bass are silent from 10.33 s, not from the sixteenth at 10.375 s.
- Filter and Riser: the sweep starts at about 10.325 s.
- Stutter: wet is 1 and dry is 0 from 10.325 s, but the loop's `delayTime` is set to the half beat only at 10.494 s. That set comes from `begin`, on another param that `resume` does not touch. Until then the loop runs at its old length, or on a first stutter at 0. In Chrome a delay inside a cycle is clamped to one render quantum, so with feedback 1 that is a 2.7 ms loop, an audible buzz at about 375 Hz, for up to half a beat. At 10.494 s the loop length then steps while the loop is audible.

Meanwhile `punchState` says the start is 10.5 s, so the pad shows "waiting" while the effect already sounds. Failing scenario: Stutter tapped in rhythm, which is how a stutter is played, or a finger that bounces on Cut. The Done-when line "each punch pad starts … on the beat or bar the spec names (tested)" is false here. The re-press test (`punch.test.ts` line 236) presses again only after the start. Fix: in `press`, when `s.start > now` (not yet started), a re-press only clears the scheduled end. Cancel the finish ramps from the old end minus its ramp onward, and keep the `begin` that is already scheduled on its line. Use `resume` only when `s.start <= now`. Add tests for the four hold pads: press, release and press again inside the guard and before the start line, with nothing moving before the start line and the stutter loop length set before wet rises.

### Should-fix

**S1. Cut does not silence the drums and the bass the Drop adds.** `src/engine/audio.ts` line 1342: Cut works only on the drums' and the bass's channel input gains. The Drop's overlay drums (open hats, ride, percussion), the bass's wide upper layer and the build's snare or clap roll go straight to the drum and duck buses (`src/engine/drop.ts` lines 247 to 261), so none of them passes those gains. Failing scenario: during a drop, or during its build, Cut leaves hats, ride, percussion, the roll and the bass's upper layer playing. Spec 9.4: "Drums and bass go silent while held." Fix: give the rack the overlay's drums and bass group gains (and the build's `fxBus`) as cut params too, or cut at a gain the overlay passes through. Test it with the drop on: every cut param is 0 on the start line.

**S2. Two animation loops, not one: the master meter on the Mix tab runs its own `requestAnimationFrame` and re-renders 15 times a second.** `src/ui/tabs/MixTab.tsx` lines 18 to 32 (`MasterMeter`). Spec 9.5 says "One animation loop drives all of it", and T2 review S2 already asked for one loop for both meters. The rest of the grep is clean. `App.tsx` line 390 and `fit.ts` line 90 are one-shot frames, and `App.tsx` line 149 is the record clock's 250 ms interval. Failing scenario: at the desk the whole Mix shows the master meter, so two rAF loops run, and the T3 measure was taken with both. Fix: draw the master meter from `startMotion`, the same way the fader meters are drawn (a transform on its fill through a data attribute, no component state), and delete the `MasterMeter` loop.

**S3. The cost measure cannot tell motion on from motion off, and the report's reading contradicts its own figures.** `tools/smoke.py` lines 3433 to 3467 (`walk_motion_cost`) and `docs/reviews/T3-measure.md`. Each part run takes one six-second sample, always motion on first and then off. The report says on and off "differ by less than the measure's own spread (… about 1.5 points)", but its two runs differ by +3.0 points (66.7 / 63.7) and −2.3 points (65.9 / 68.2). My run of the cost part reads 78.4 % with motion on against 71.7 % off (+6.7 points). With motion on it also had a 51 ms long task, a 62.9 ms worst frame gap and 336 frames against 361. Off it had no long task and a 44.1 ms gap. The worst render callback is 21.9 to 48.5 ms against a 5 ms callback in every reading, on and off, so that figure shows glitches in the container rather than anything about motion, and the report does not say so. Nothing is hidden, but "the movement adds no load … that this measure can see" is not supported. Three runs, two of them higher with motion on, are as consistent with a cost of a few points as with none. Fix: alternate on and off, at least three times each (as `tools/cost.py` took the median of 3 for P10 S4), and report the median and the spread of each. Reword the reading to what the numbers allow, and include this run's figures.

### Minor

**M1. After a Stop during a Wash, the wash reverb is never cut from its send.** `src/engine/punch.ts` lines 212 and 343 to 347. `reset` bumps every pad's `gen`, so the pending unlink sees a newer gen and does nothing. The reverb stays linked until the next Wash tap. Its input is a gain at 0, so the cost is likely small in Chrome, but the claim "idle, the reverb is cut off" is false after Stop. Fix: `reset` unlinks the wash itself, after its tail rings out.

**M2. The idle rack is not free, and not quite transparent.** `src/engine/punch.ts` lines 117 to 131: with no pad held, the stutter's 2 s delay line still records the full mix (`rec` at 1), and the 20 kHz low-pass processes it. Web Audio reads a low-pass Q in dB, and 0.9 dB gives a peak of +1.9 dB near 19 kHz at 44.1 and 48 kHz (+0.1 to +0.2 dB at 10 kHz). That is in series with the master's own 20 kHz low-pass. Fix: Q at −3 dB (no peak), and `rec` at 0 while Stutter is idle. That needs the loop filled before its start line, so set `rec` to 1 one half beat before the start, which the guard allows only for a start at least a half beat ahead. Alternatively, record the cost as a choice.

**M3. The first Wash tap builds its 3.5 s impulse and sets the convolver after the start line is chosen.** `src/engine/punch.ts` lines 180 to 183, 338, 351 to 358 and 377 to 387. The start can be as little as 25 ms ahead (`PUNCH_GUARD`). If the stereo noise loop and Chrome's convolver setup take longer than that on the phone, the send's opening ramp is scheduled in the past and jumps. This was not measured. Fix: build the tail when the rack is made, or on idle after load, or choose the start after `linkWash`.

**M4. A punch press during a drop stops the Drop's level follower for the rest of that drop.** `src/engine/index.ts` line 638: `note()` marks `dropHand` for any op, `punch` included, so an Echo tap freezes it. A Cut held from the build into the drop is read by the follower as a quiet section, because the follower hears the channels after Cut. Neither case is tested. Fix: leave `punch` out of `dropHand`, and pause the follower's readings only while Cut is in. Alternatively, record the freeze as a choice.

**M5. Logging is claimed but not tested.** The `t3.test.ts` header says "a press is logged in a take", but no test records a take and finds `punch` events in it. The event format replays as `engine.punch(pad, down)`, which is right, but nothing checks it. Fix: arm a take, press and lift a pad, and read the take's events.

**M6. The grid the rack lands on is not tested against what is heard.** `src/engine/audio.ts` lines 1337 to 1342 read the transport's ticks. Every timing test takes a hand-made `PunchGrid`, so the "on the audio clock" proof assumes that beat 0 of the transport is a bar line of the sequencer and that `bpm.value` is constant up to the end line. A tempo ramp (a vibe landing at another bpm) moves the lines. Fix: in `walk_punch`, check that Cut's `end` is a whole bar of `Tone.getTransport().getTicksAtTime(end)`, and that the bar `position()` reports changes there.

**M7. Under Still the loop keeps running each frame.** `src/ui/motion.ts` lines 130 to 175: it runs `querySelectorAll(MOVERS)` per frame, and the meter and ghost queries 15 times a second. Spec 9.5 allows this ("can be switched off" is met visually: no pulse, no ghost, the root `still`). Record it, or skip the frame's work when still and nothing changed.

**M8. The ghosts only approximate.** `src/ui/motion.ts` line 151 takes the LFO ghost's phase from `position().bar`, while the engine's LFO runs on transport beats from bar 1. `punchSweep` (`src/ui/logic.ts` line 897) is linear in time, while the filter moves exponentially in Hz, toward 280 Hz, which is not the master Filter dial's lowest point. A re-press keeps the old start, so the ghost jumps. These are cosmetic. Fix: record them as approximations, or derive both ghosts from the engine's own values.

**M9. Small guards.** `src/engine/punch.ts` lines 61 to 67: the fallback for browsers without `cancelAndHoldAtTime` sets `p.value` (the value now) at a future time, which is a step. `src/ui/punch.tsx` relies on `pointercancel` to let a held pad go when the app is backgrounded. A `visibilitychange` that lets every held pad go would guard against a stuck Cut.

## Choices for Pedro to veto

- Start lines, where the spec is silent: Cut, Filter and Riser on the next sixteenth; Stutter on the next half beat; Echo and Wash on the next beat. A press within 25 ms of a line goes to the line after it.
- Filter is a separate low-pass in the punch rack (a 2-beat sweep from 20 kHz to 280 Hz), not the master Filter dial's filter. The dial shows a ghost.
- Echo is a dotted eighth with feedback 0.5. Wash is a 3.5 s seeded noise reverb. Riser climbs for 8 beats to its peak and falls over one beat.
- A second tap of Echo or Wash while it runs keeps it in and moves its end one bar on from the new beat.
- A finger that slides off a pad keeps it held until it lifts (pointer capture).
- In Simple the Filter pad reads "Sweep" (Simple shows no Advanced words). Advanced uses the spec's names.
- Cut leaves the sends' tails ringing (they come after the channel input), and the kick's pump keeps ducking while the kick is cut.
- The phone's Punch page sits second in the Mix chips, after Master.

## Fixer notes

- **E1, fixed.** `src/engine/punch.ts` `press`: a pad counts as started only once its start line has passed. A second tap of Echo or Wash before its start beat keeps the opening ramp on its line and moves only the close (held at the old end minus its ramp, then `landAt` the new end). For Filter it clears from the end minus its 20 ms ramp, and the sweep to the floor goes on if the release cut it short. For Riser it clears the fall, the climb goes on to its top, and a fresh noise source takes over before the start line, because a source cannot be un-stopped. `resume` (land now) runs only once the start has passed.
