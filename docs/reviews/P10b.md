## Findings

### Errors

**E1. dubTechno's width collapses in the first wind-down bar; drop-3 fails in both runs.** `src/music/drop.ts` (`windSteps`, levels on `share * share`, spread on `share`); measured by `tools/dropcheck.py:519`. Measured 0.126 > 0.071 with the section at 0.063: 13% of the extra width left after bar 1 against the check's 15% (other vibes 17% to 50%, so twoStep is close too). The cause is in the shape: the layers' level and their pan spread fall together, so their side energy falls with level squared times spread squared, mostly in bar 1, while the layers' own level falls only about 4 dB in bar 1 and then about 17 dB in bar 2 (spec 8.9 says "an even step in loudness"; the numbers are not even). Fix: choose the level and spread curves so the layers' side energy (and their level in dB) fall an even share a bar, for example spread on `sqrt(share)` or level on `share` rather than `share²`, update `windSteps`, its tests and the spec sentence to match; do not change `WIND_NOT_FIRST`. Confirm with three runs of drop-3 in a row.

**E2. The 4-bar wind-down is never measured in the browser.** `tools/dropcheck.py:386` (`one_drop`) and `:610`, `:651` set `wind: 2` for every vibe, yet nine vibes' own recipes wind down over 4 bars (deepTechno, melodicTechno, dubTechno, edm, trance, progressive, futureGarage, ambient, drone), and those are the defaults Pedro hears (C21 was heard on the live build). The 4-bar path (bar index 2 and 3 in `windBar`, the held notes' order, the voice allowance over four bars) is only unit-tested. Fix: run `one_drop` at each vibe's own wind length (drop `wind: 2` from the `setDrop` at line 386 and let the recipe's value stand), or add one 4-bar run per drop group; keep every threshold.

**E3. drumAndBass landed 2.5 to 2.8 dB under its section in one of two runs.** `tools/dropcheck.py:528-535` caught it. The extra layers were already at -120 dB, so the music itself was quieter after the end, not the overlay. This matches C20's unsettled reading (DubTechno -4.07 dB, Drone -3.79 dB after a fresh browser, "the bass lost again"). Cause not found here. Fix: probe the instruments' own levels on the two bars after the wind-down (bass first) over repeated drop-3 runs; check whether the end bar line leaves the master pump deeper (`windBar`'s `setMix("pump", step.pump)` against the restore in the end branch of `src/engine/index.ts`) or whether a let-go of the held bass double (`src/engine/index.ts:1362`) cancels the section's sub. If it is C20's cause and not P10b's, record that under Carried; the smoke still has to pass three runs in a row.

### Should-fix

**S1. The first wind-down bar can start from a stale value.** `src/engine/drop.ts:384-404`. `wind()` reads `out.gain.value` and `comp.gain.value` when the bar callback runs (the look-ahead before `t`), and uses `this.spreadNow` (a target, not where the pans are). Events scheduled between now and `t` are not cancelled (`cancelScheduledValues(max(t, now))`), so a Width move or a follower reading in that last fraction of a second (`setTargetAtTime`, tau 0.05 or 0.15 s) keeps moving the param up to `t`, where `setValueAtTime(start, t)` steps it back: a small level step (a possible click) and a descent starting from the pre-move value; the pans start from the new spread while the levels start from the old. In the plain case (no knob move near the bar line) the start value is right, and the fix for the Chrome near-0 start (3d82690) is sound. Fix: keep each overlay param's last aim (value, start time, tau) in JS where `level()`, `width()`, `setComp()` and `impact()` set it, and compute the value at `t` from it (`aim + (v0 - aim) * exp(-(t - t0) / tau)`) as the ramp's start; or ignore `level()`/`width()`/`setComp()` from the press on (`dropper` intent off), not only once `wind()` has run.

**S2. Ambient and Drone skip the song-unchanged check.** `tools/dropcheck.py:555`. The document is compared two bars after the end, when Morph has resumed and written by itself (spec 8.11: a drop holds Morph from the press to its end), so the skip is explained, but it leaves the Done-when line untested in the two vibes whose drop is a bloom with long held notes. A safer check that loosens nothing: read the document on the wind-down's end bar line (before Morph's first move after it), or compare the event log from the press to the end and require that every entry in it is marked as Morph's (and none from the drop). Apply it to all 24.

**S3. The width check reads the master, with a tolerance, rather than the layers' own width.** `tools/dropcheck.py:508` lets the master width rise by up to 0.01 a bar and still count as falling; the Done-when says width falls in steps. `dropLayers()` already returns the layers' own width, free of the section's bar-to-bar stray. Fix: also require the layers' own width (`b["l"]["width"]`) to fall strictly every wind-down bar; keep the master check as it is.

**S4. The Filter check runs in one vibe (deepHouse, drop-1) only.** `tools/dropcheck.py:248-278`. The path is vibe-independent (every recipe, a bloom included, sets `plan.impact` and `dropMixOn`; `src/music/drop.ts:218`), so one vibe proves the logic. It does not prove a 4-bar wind-down (the walk uses wind 2, `tools/dropcheck.py:106`) or a bloom. Fix: run the same reads once with `wind: 4` in a bloom vibe (Ambient, drop-6), so "not before the end" is shown across four bars. Note for the changelog: a Filter move during the drop still acts at once (P10 review E1, "knobs still work"); what comes back on the last bar line is the value set before the drop, which is what the check tests.

**S5. "The drop's last bar is wider than the section" applies `WIDER` to a single, partial bar.** `tools/dropcheck.py:512`. `WIDER` (1.15) is the existing threshold for the drop's eight-bar mean; on the one window from the press to the first wind-down bar line it failed twoStep in run 2 (0.294 against 0.256 x 1.15 = 0.294) while twoStep's own drop mean is 1.21x. Fix: require `wid[0] > sec["width"]` and `wid[0]` within the drop's own bar-to-bar stray of its eight-bar mean, or read the drop's last full bar before the press; record the change in the changelog. Do not lower `WIDER`.

### Minor

**M1. Spec and code differ on the held notes.** Spec 8.9 (`docs/SPEC.md:323`) says the held notes' doubles leave "one instrument at a time"; with four held and two bars, `windSteps` gives 4 > 3 > 1 > 0 (`src/music/wind.test.ts:91`), two leaving together in bar 2. Fix: say "at least one instrument a bar" in the spec, or keep one a bar and let the rest go on the end bar line.

**M2. `WIND_STEP_DB` (0.5 dB a bar) is far below what is measured (4 to 20 dB a bar).** `tools/dropcheck.py:40`. It passes, but a wind-down that fell 1 dB in its first bar and the rest at the end would also pass the level line. Fix: once E1 is fixed, set it near half the smallest measured step (new constant, not an existing threshold) and record the value.

**M3. The two-bar landing mean is skipped with Morph on, and Ambient would fail it.** `tools/dropcheck.py:534`. Ambient landed +1.34 and +1.26 dB (mean about +1.3 dB, over `LOUD_DB`); each bar passes only because Ambient's own bar-to-bar stray is 1.32 dB. Not shown to be a defect (Ambient's section moves by 1.3 dB from bar to bar), but the exception hides it. Safer: compare the two bars after with the section's bars at the same place in its loop (from `sec_bars`), or with the mean only when `morphPlan()` shows no instrument out in those bars; apply to all 24.

**M4. Electro rides 0.7 to 1.2 dB under its section through the wind-down and after.** Within the per-bar tolerance; listed so Pedro can listen for a dip in Electro's wind-down.

## Other checks

- Audio clock: every wind-down step is scheduled at `t + (i + 1) * bar` from the bar line; the voice allowance (`room(t)`) and the pump step act per bar on audio time. Pump depth is a value read per kick, so setting it in the bar callback applies from that bar line's kick.
- Stuck notes: none (`live_after` 0 in all eight vibes run); voices fall strictly every bar.
- Clicks: none over `CLICK_FACTOR` in the wind-down or the two bars after, in drop-3 and drop-6.
- Seeded random: no `Math.random` added. UI never imports Tone: no file under `src/ui/` changed.
- 48 px controls: no UI change, unaffected.
- Spec 8.9 matches the code except M1 and the "even step in loudness" sentence (E1).
- Is the calm-down there in the numbers: yes for the overlay. The layers alone fall about 4 then 15 to 20 dB a bar and are silent a bar after; voices fall 27-37 > 21-28 > 7-12 > 0-6; the master width falls to the section's. The master's level holds the section's throughout (within about 1 dB, by design: the follower keeps it), so the calm-down is in width and density, not loudness. Most of the change lands in bar 1 for the width and in bar 2 for the layers' level (E1). Whether it feels like a calm-down is Pedro's ear.
