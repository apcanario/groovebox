## Errors

**E1. The Filter knob does nothing for the whole drop.** `src/engine/index.ts` 540 (`if (this.dropMixOn && key === "sweep") return this.changed();`).
- Spec 8.9, Rules: "Knobs still work during a drop." While the drop is on, a Filter or Tone move is stored and its dial moves, but the sound does not change until the wind-down ends. That is a dead control for as long as the drop lasts, and drops are meant to stay on.
- The spec's "fully open filter" is what the drop does when it arrives. It does not forbid a hand from moving the knob afterwards.
- **Fix:** open the filter at the impact as now. From then on, a hand move of `sweep` acts on the graph (`this.graph?.setMix(key, v)`) for the rest of the drop. At the end, return to the knob's value as now. Add a smoke check: during a drop, Filter to -0.8 lowers the master's top end (or `graph.lp.frequency`), and after the wind-down the value is the knob's.

## Should fix

**S1. You can hear the level follower move, and it starts every first drop 1.8 dB down.** `src/engine/index.ts` 228 (`compSteady = 0.8`), 1154, 1252; `src/engine/drop.ts` 271.
- At the impact bar line the overlay gain jumps (tau 10 ms) to `compSteady`, which is 0.8 (-1.9 dB) on the first drop of a session. The follower then corrects by up to 3 dB a beat.
- Measured: EDM gain 0.82 -> 0.95 -> 1.02 -> 0.98 over the first three bars of the drop, a rise of 1.9 dB. Ambient is a bloom with no impact to mask the step: its first drop bar is -1.44 dB against the section, then it creeps back over three bars (gain 0.81 -> 0.85 -> 0.92 -> 0.95).
- The smoke test misses all of this. It skips the impact bar and averages the next four bars.
- Once settled, the follower still moves the gain every beat with no deadband, ±0.4 dB bar to bar in my probe.
- **Fix:**
  - Start the follower at 1.0 (or at the last steady gain of the same vibe), never a fixed 0.8.
  - For a bloom, do not set the gain at the arrival bar line at all.
  - Limit the correction to 0.5 dB a beat after the first bar of the drop, and ignore errors under 0.3 dB.
  - In `drop_vibes`, also fail when any single drop bar after the impact bar is more than 1.5 dB from the section.

**S2. The follower works against hand moves during the drop.** `src/engine/index.ts` 1234-1256.
- It aims the master at the energy the section had before the build, every beat, for the whole drop and wind-down, over a range of -14 dB to +2 dB (`setComp` clamps to 0.2..1.25).
- Turn an instrument's level up, switch an instrument on, or raise Saturation during the drop: within one or two beats the whole mix is pulled back down. Switch an instrument off: the rest is pushed up by as much as 2 dB. That goes against "Knobs still work during a drop".
- **Fix:** let the follower settle over the first two bars of the drop, then freeze it. Or re-take the reference whenever a level, toggle or mix change lands during the drop. Either way, add a test: a level dial raised 6 dB during the drop raises the master by at least 3 dB four beats later.

**S3. "The master never clips" is proved with less than 1 dB to spare, and only over part of the drop.** `tools/dropcheck.py` 278-305; `src/engine/drop.ts` 297-312.
- The highest peak is -0.7 to -0.8 dBFS, and it comes from the impact: the sub at 0.35 and the burst join after the follower, straight into the limiter. The limiter is a compressor with a 1 ms attack (C1), not a ceiling, and in G1 it let +0.2 dBFS through.
- The check reads only the section, the build plus the impact, and four drop bars. It never reads the wind-down, the bar line where the drop ends, Width at 1, the Hold cliffhanger, or Saturation up. `p1 = p2` (line 284) is dead.
- **Fix:**
  - Take the impact voices 2 dB down, or put them before `comp`, so the follower hears them.
  - Extend the peak read through the wind-down and two bars after it.
  - Add one vibe at Width 1, Hold, and Saturation 0.6.
  - Fail above -0.5 dBFS rather than at 0, so a small regression shows up before it clips.

**S4. The overlay's cost is measured on a light scene only. Ambient's held notes overflow the cap.** `tools/cost.py` `drops`; `src/engine/index.ts` 1180 and 1615; `src/engine/drop.ts` 23.
- The drop adds 5 to 10 points of load (my figures above). Spec 8.8 sets the ceiling Pedro accepted: a heavy six-slot scene measures 71.5%, and 72 to 78% is "too much for a phone". A drop on that scene would sit at about 77 to 81%. Nobody has measured it.
- Trance fills the 48-voice cap. Ambient goes over it by 5 notes in every run. Those notes are not played, so part of a held pad's doubling is missing for a bar.
- The cause is S8's bar-by-bar re-doubling. Each bar line starts a fresh set of voices while the last set rings out for about 0.8 s.
- **Fix:**
  - Fix S8 first. It removes most of the bar-line overlap.
  - Then measure the drop on `all,fx6max` (or the heaviest scene `cost.py` has) and report the figure in spec 8.9's cost line.
  - If that is above 71%, cut the synth layer from three oscillators per note to two, or lower `VOICE_CAP`, and put the figure to Pedro.
  - The builder's 75% and 45 ms should not go into the changelog without saying the machine was busy.

**S5. Drum and bass passes the 1 dB line with no margin.** `tools/dropcheck.py` 300.
- -1.00 dB in my run. The follower reported +0.15 dB for the same drop, so the follower's reading and the test's disagree by about 1.1 dB.
- The likely cause: D&B's break varies over more than the 16-beat window that the per-beat reference keys on. The follower then compares the drop's beats with the wrong reference beats.
- The next run can fail on noise alone.
- **Fix:** key the reference by the section's own loop length (chord cycle or 8 bars, whichever is longer), not a fixed 16 beats. Then show at least 0.3 dB of margin in every vibe over two full runs.

**S6. Advanced Play puts DROP between the grid and the strip.** `src/ui/tabs/PlayTab.tsx` 95.
- Spec 8.6: "On the Play tab the grid sits directly on top of it and uses it as its column labels". The strip is indented to line up under the grid's columns. In `360x640-advanced-play.png`, a 56 px DROP tile now separates the grid from its labels.
- Spec 8.9 places DROP between the instruments and the strip only in Simple. For Advanced it says "the same button".
- **Fix:** in Advanced, render the drop row above the grid (under the header), or make it the grid's bottom row only if the strip stays directly under the grid's columns. Add a frame-walk check that the strip's top meets the grid's bottom on Advanced Play.

**S7. "Loudness" is unweighted RMS in both the follower and the test.** `src/engine/continuity.ts` (GrooveEnergy), `tools/dropcheck.py` 47.
- RMS is dominated by the kick and the bass. The drop adds mostly upper-mid and high content (octave-up layers, hats, ride, burst), which unweighted RMS under-reads. A drop that measures equal can sound louder. Spec 8.9 says "Nothing gets turned up".
- **Fix:** put a K-weighting pre-filter (two biquads: a high shelf of +4 dB above 1.5 kHz and a high-pass at 38 Hz, as in ITU-R BS.1770) in front of the energy worklet used by the follower and the smoke test. Re-tune `DROP_TYPICAL` from the new readings.

**S8. Held notes are doubled bar by bar, so they dip at every bar line.** `src/engine/index.ts` 1180 and 1615; `src/engine/drop.ts` 392-419.
- A held chord, pad or drone gets a fresh voice at each bar line: 0.25 s linear attack, from a new oscillator whose phase does not match the old one. The previous voice ends 20 ms after the line and releases with tau 0.15 s.
- At equal pitch, two voices at an arbitrary phase offset sum anywhere between partial cancellation and full level. Their summed power about 0.1 s after the line is about -3 dB. So the doubled layer swells at the bar rate, at a different depth each bar.
- In Ambient and Drone, where the whole drop is held notes, that is a pulse at the bar rate. The overlap is also what pushes Ambient over the cap (S4).
- I did not measure the dip itself; this comes from the envelope maths.
- **Fix:** give each held note one overlay voice that lives until the note lets go (the `sustain` entry is deleted) or the drop ends. Stop it then with the normal release. Add a smoke check: on Drone during the drop, the per-beat energy at bar lines is within 1 dB of mid-bar.

**S9. Some of the logic has no test.** `src/ui/logic.ts` 91, 741, 748; `src/engine/index.ts` `drop()` (the disarm branch), `switchSection` (the drop branch), `setMix` during the drop.
- `dropSegments`, `dropLabel` and the "After drop" word of `stripState` are pure UI logic with no unit test, against the rule that pure logic has unit tests.
- Untested in both unit and smoke tests:
  - a disarm (second press before the bar line) moving a held section into the ordinary next-bar queue;
  - tapping the playing section during the drop to take a waiting one back;
  - Pump and Filter returning to the knob values on the graph after the wind-down. The document check cannot see the graph.
- **Fix:**
  - Unit tests for `dropSegments` (armed 4, build 8 bars counting 4-4-3-3-2-2-1-1, on 4, wind, off 0), `dropLabel` (each phase and intent), and `stripState` with `afterDrop`.
  - Smoke assertions for the disarm path, the take-back tap, and `graph.lp.frequency`/pump depth after the wind-down equal to before the press.

**S10. The 0.2 ms drum retry hides an unexplained error instead of fixing it.** `src/engine/audio.ts` 692-702.
- Tone's assertion (`Source.js` 133) compares a start with the source's *previous start*, not with a stop. So the comment's explanation ("its noise source's stop at exactly the next start") does not match the error.
- `AttackQueue` (`attacks.ts`) already hands every start of a source a time at least 0.1 ms after its last start. The error should be impossible. Something starts a drum source outside the queue, or at a time the queue never saw. The cause is unknown.
- The retry has side effects:
  - When the throw comes from `noise.start` after `envelope.triggerAttack` has already run, the envelope gets triggered a second time.
  - The queue's `last` never learns about the +0.2 ms.
  - A second throw still escapes the scheduler.
  - It is silent, so the next run cannot tell whether it fired.
- **Fix:** log the voice, the time, the queue's last start for the source and `Tone`'s state time in the `catch`, and count the retries in `dropInfo` or a drums debug getter. Have the smoke test fail if the count is above 0, then find the real cause: suspect an attack started outside `atTime` around the drop's `dropReset` / vibe landings in `drop_vibes`. Keep a guard only once the cause is understood and written in the comment.

## Minor

**M1. The overlay's cap is not a hard cap.** `src/engine/drop.ts` 224, 295, 309. The riser, the two impact sweeps and the impact sub call `track` without `room()`, so up to 4 voices go past `VOICE_CAP`. **Fix:** check `room()` before each, or write in the comment that the cap counts only layer and roll voices.

**M2. A press during the wind-down does nothing, and the spec does not say so.** `src/music/drop.ts` 188. The choice is reasonable and tested, but it is a rule the spec lacks. **Fix:** add one line to spec 8.9 ("A press during the wind-down does nothing; press again once it ends") so the next row does not reopen it. The main chat edits the spec.

**M3. The spec edit names the wrong exception.** `docs/SPEC.md` 259. It says "a second exception to the 10 px between groups", but the vibe picker's exception (8.6) is to the 2 px tile gap, not to the 10 px group gap. This is the first exception to 10 px. The edit also leaves out the 4 px top padding (`src/style.css`, `.shell.now .body`). The exception itself is justified: at 360 x 640, the lanes would otherwise drop under 48 px. **Fix:** "(the one exception to the 10 px between groups of section 8.12; the body's top padding is 4 px)".

**M4. A bloom's reverb collapses at its peak.** `src/engine/drop.ts` 385. Ambient and Drone have `parts.reverb`, so the send grows over the eight bars, then `normalAt` takes it back to 0 (tau 0.4 s) on the bar line where the bloom arrives. Spec 8.10 calls for "the pads open and widen over 8 bars", so the arrival should not lose space. **Fix:** for a bloom, keep the send through the drop and ramp it out over the wind-down.

**M5. Hold can silently become Gap.** `src/engine/drop.ts` 258; `src/engine/index.ts` (`lastChord`). With the synth off in the section (and no held layer note yet), `lastChord` is empty or left over from another section, so Hold hangs nothing or a stale chord. **Fix:** take the chord from the section's current chord (the sequencer's chord at the bar), not the last synth event.

**M6. A recipe comment says something the engine does not do.** `src/styles/trap.ts` 25 says "a hat-and-snare roll", but the roll is one snare/clap voice. **Fix:** reword it to "a snare roll".

**M7. Dead code in the smoke test.** `tools/dropcheck.py` 284, `p1 = p2`. **Fix:** remove it, or read the build separately from the impact as the docstring says.
